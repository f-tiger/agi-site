# Product Marketing Context

**Document version:** v2
**Last updated:** 2026-10-01

## Product and category

BPJ Quote Studio turns a service provider's rates and scope into a portable interactive quote tool. It is the first narrow experiment in a possible customer-facing microapp platform. It is a free browser tool deployed on BPJ, not yet SaaS and not an AI generation service.

## Audience and job hypotheses

Initial distribution focuses on freelance video editors and small video studios with a current real client quote and repeat package changes. Web/content templates remain available, but their acquisition fit has not been tested. The provider creates the tool and may pay for future cloud management; the provider's customer adjusts scope for free. A recipient is not automatically a future creator. Personas, pain frequency, support cost and willingness to pay are unverified.

## Alternatives and switching

Spreadsheets, chat messages, forms, Tally, Outgrow and general app builders. The prototype's portable file, itemized arithmetic and simple service scope may help, but there are no comparative user results. Habit and anxiety include clients avoiding unfamiliar tools and providers already having a working quote process. No customer quotes or testimonials have been collected.

## Positioning and differentiation hypothesis

“Let clients choose the scope and understand the estimate before work begins.” Sell a clear task, not an AI label. The initial distinction from BPJ's existing quote comparison tool is seller-created client interaction versus buyer-side supplier comparison. No claims of more sales, time saved or market-standard prices.

## Offer

The deployed tool is free, with local draft/JSON, shareable customer links and HTML export. A visitor can try the customer view immediately without signup, then enter their own prices. Proposed future $19/month creator and $49/month studio offers are research hypotheses; not for sale now. Cloud projects, versions, domains, team permissions, subscriptions and payment acceptance are not built. Do not advertise them as existing features or collect payment for them.

## Voice, objections and proof

Calm, practical, specific, bilingual. Be explicit about offline files, public configuration links and excluded tax. Sample prices are fictional. No verified customers, demand, retention, revenue or acquisition interest. The proof available this turn is working software and recorded technical tests only. No claims that a template brand or service provider is verified by BPJ.

## Goal and decision

Test real customer handover and a second task before expanding. First decision sample: 10 qualified creators, 5 completing their own setup, 3 using it for actual client handover, 3 using it on a second task. Insufficient reach remains insufficient evidence. Real payment and positive contribution are later gates for SaaS, not automatically implied by passing the free-use gate. Source and full experiment definition: `docs/base44-build-grow-exit-2026-10-01.md` and the fleet bets register.

## Distribution and measurement hypothesis

Lead with the specific objection: “Can we add another version?” Show the scope and estimate changing before asking a creator to enter rates. The bilingual video-quote landing provides six scoping questions and links to a working demo. Existing home/video/discovery/search surfaces route relevant visitors into it. Public recommendation links and branded preview images are implemented; neither proves referral growth.

2026-09-01–09-28 settled GSC baseline for the entire BPJ property: 704 impressions, 1 click, 0.142% CTR. The quote tool launched October 1, so this is the existing distribution baseline, not product demand evidence. First-party `/api/reach` counts use a different window and event definition. Do not derive a conversion rate by dividing them.

Observe anonymous action counts: entry, preview, own edit/confirmation, generated file/copied link, client open, copied summary, remix and clean tool-link copy. Campaign labels are allowlisted but user-editable. These are neither people nor a joined funnel. The existing daily reach snapshot preserves these signals; failures are null, not zero. Qualified creator, actual handover and second-task evidence remain separate from telemetry. No community post, direct outreach, advertisement or customer recruitment is claimed as completed.
