# Product Marketing Context

**Document version:** v1
**Last updated:** 2026-10-01

## Product and category

BPJ Quote Studio turns a service provider's rates and scope into a portable interactive quote tool. It is the first narrow experiment in a possible customer-facing microapp platform. It is a browser tool prototype, not yet SaaS and not an AI generation service.

## Audience and job hypotheses

Small video/web/content studios with a current real client quote and repeat package changes. The provider creates the tool and may pay for future cloud management; the provider's customer adjusts scope for free. A recipient is not automatically a future creator. Personas, pain frequency, support cost and willingness to pay are unverified.

## Alternatives and switching

Spreadsheets, chat messages, forms, Tally, Outgrow and general app builders. The prototype's portable file, itemized arithmetic and simple service scope may help, but there are no comparative user results. Habit and anxiety include clients avoiding unfamiliar tools and providers already having a working quote process. No customer quotes or testimonials have been collected.

## Positioning and differentiation hypothesis

“Let clients choose the scope and understand the estimate before work begins.” Sell a clear task, not an AI label. The initial distinction from BPJ's existing quote comparison tool is seller-created client interaction versus buyer-side supplier comparison. No claims of more sales, time saved or market-standard prices.

## Offer

The working prototype is free, with local draft/JSON and HTML export. Proposed future $19/month creator and $49/month studio offers are research hypotheses; not for sale now. Cloud projects, versions, domains, team permissions, subscriptions and payment acceptance are not built. Do not advertise them as existing features or collect payment for them.

## Voice, objections and proof

Calm, practical, specific, bilingual. Be explicit about offline files, public configuration links and excluded tax. Sample prices are fictional. No verified customers, demand, retention, revenue or acquisition interest. The proof available this turn is working software and recorded technical tests only. No claims that a template brand or service provider is verified by BPJ.

## Goal and decision

Test real customer handover and a second task before expanding. First decision sample: 10 qualified creators, 5 completing their own setup, 3 using it for actual client handover, 3 using it on a second task. Insufficient reach remains insufficient evidence. Real payment and positive contribution are later gates for SaaS, not automatically implied by passing the free-use gate.
