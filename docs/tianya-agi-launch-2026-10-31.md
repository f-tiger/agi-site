# Tianya Echo joins AGI

Owner request: unify the Tianya site's style with AGI and publish on AGI to grow registered users and revenue. The public entry points are `/tianya` and `/zh/tianya`; the full Chinese archive is `/zh/tianya-research`. These are AGI routes, not a new domain.

## Product and commercial decisions

A visitor can turn one assumption into a startup pilot or paper-position plan, include a counter-case/review date, compare costs and profit, and keep review notes. Free explicit local storage and JSON export/import remain useful without signup. Inputs stay in the browser until the visitor explicitly hands a draft to the member tab and clicks Save there.

The free Fleet registration provides the established community/Jarvis identity. It does not unlock paid cloud storage. The existing independent-key membership adds 50 cloud workspaces, 10 versions each, 64 KiB/version, 5 MiB total, at 9 USDT/30 days plus matching decimal and network fees. No new price, automatic renewal, extra AI quota, broker integration or recurring task is introduced. The member key needs a separate backup.

Catalog product `tianya-validation` attributes actual checkout orders to this tool. Backend payment/entitlement/isolation tests use mocked chain receipts and are not real payments. The page must never count a popup, draft handoff or synthetic acknowledgement as a purchase. Successful saves require same origin, exact popup, nonce, product and a valid revision context; current edits are compared with the sent snapshot.

No public community messages, ads, paid APIs or marketing enrolment.

GA4 uses the existing registered AGI property and shared default-on runtime, retaining explicit opt-out, DNT/GPC and QA exclusions. The only added business inputs are fixed event names: calculate/local_save/export separated into demo/own, and member_open/register_open/research_open/restore/import. No title, thesis, counter-case, financial input, review notes, filename or URL query is accepted in event details. Free-account pages and member portals retain private-route exclusions.

Review first useful own-input calculation/export, return use, server-created non-test accounts and product-attributed paid receipts separately after launch. Reconcile settled payments, refunds, fees and support costs before reporting profit. Do not divide unrelated anonymous visit/action counts by fleet-wide accounts to invent conversion. There are no measured revenue or registration gains from this release yet.

## Three passes and two challenges

1. Outcome: an existing-domain product that can earn registration and payment intent, not just an archive reskin.
2. Constraints: preserve evidence dates, existing free access, existing pricing/identity contract and public-repository privacy.
3. Acceptance: bilingual live pages, mobile usability, working calculators/backup, real member handoff, analytics/SEO, successful deployment and read-only production checks.

Challenge one: free registration is not cloud membership. The interface names the two identities rather than granting or advertising nonexistent entitlement. Full Fleet-paid identity bridging was deferred because account deletion/recovery and key rotation require a separate lifecycle design.

Challenge two: improved recurring cost is not profit, a planned exit is not a maximum realized loss, and historic folklore is not calibrated prediction. The actual calculator displays setup-inclusive profit and paper loss assumptions. The strongest commercial counter-case remains free JSON sufficiency and crypto/key friction; observe these before expanding features.
