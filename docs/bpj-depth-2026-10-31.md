## Two adversarial self-reviews
- Utility/cold start: another account CTA or generic directory would duplicate existing functions. The Kimi panel supplies practical self-check steps and a reusable prompt; manju supplies explicit want/watching/watched states independently of existing favorite intent. No claim BPJ executes Kimi or streams videos.
- False success/privacy: checked steps are self-reports; watch states are not playback. Saving/return-link clicks/download initiation do not prove retention or revenue. No task choice, checked step, work ID, status or input is sent in the new measurement payload. Broken local storage is not silently overwritten; changes from another tab are detected. Existing browser QA/DNT/GPC/opt-out behavior is preserved.

## Free and commercial value
Free Kimi steps/prompt/export work without signup. The already-implemented account offer saves Kimi to the cross-device followed-tool list and exposes recorded changes; the new task steps do not sync. The manju progress list is local, with text export and an explicit continue link; accounts do not sync it, and RSS is catalogue updates, not episode alerts. Existing vendor verification/inquiry services remain the commercial path, but this retention experiment has no proven causal revenue link or new paid offer. Do not infer willingness to pay from engagement.
