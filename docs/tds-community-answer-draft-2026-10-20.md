## Draft A — “Is there a SMISKI checklist PDF?”

The Doll Scout has a free, source-linked SMISKI Museum checklist here: https://thedollscout.com/series/smiski-museum

Mark the styles you own, keep an extra-copy quantity, and use the collection tracker to manage owned and wanted figures. If you need a paper copy, open the printable trading-meetup worksheet at https://thedollscout.com/collecting/trading-meetup-checklist and choose **Print → Save as PDF** in your browser’s print dialog. It is a printable web page, not a pre-generated PDF download. The blank worksheet does not read or change your saved collection. The site is independent and is not endorsed by SMISKI.

Official naming reference: https://smiski.com/e/products/museumseries/

## Draft B — “Where is the Sonny Angel value calculator?”

A rarity or probability calculator is not a resale-price estimator. The Doll Scout can help you track owned/wanted styles and plan a budget using your own figures, but it does not currently provide a verified live market valuation. Do not infer a figure’s selling price from rarity alone: condition, region, packaging, and actual comparable sales matter. For current product information, start with the official series catalog: https://www.sonnyangel.com/en/products/
