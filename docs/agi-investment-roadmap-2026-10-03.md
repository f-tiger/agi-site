## Two adversarial self-checks

A: Famous speakers are industry participants, and agreement can reflect shared incentives. AI demand can grow while returns fall because of valuation, cyclicality, dilution, capex or competition. Every route has an explicit failure condition. MU peak earnings are not normalized earnings; NOW ACV is not recognized revenue; PANW total ARR is not AI-agent security revenue; world-model demos are not proven business results.

B: Dynamic metadata and notification bugs can misrepresent new evidence. Enforce versioned content hashes, date/identity validation and rollback rejection. Historical/backfilled videos do not trigger new signals; new leads are bounded to one daily digest. Owner commands require a verified webhook plus the configured private owner chat. Reuse the existing serialized Durable Object with separate state, persist pending leads before sending, and require Telegram acknowledgement. Exactly-once delivery is not guaranteed across a crash between external acknowledgement and durable storage. Local watchlists do not configure Telegram alerts.
