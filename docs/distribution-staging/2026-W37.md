## ⚠️ 先读:这几稿为什么这样写(owner 2026-09-06:「提示内容是AI生成,你要人性化的表达,不然被封了」)

r/incremental_games 这类版块对 AI 味的文案非常敏感,判定往往在读完前三行就完成了。第一版草稿
被 owner 一眼看穿,原因很具体,记下来避免以后再犯:

**AI 味的几个硬特征(改稿时逐条查)**
1. **破折号成串**。`——` / `—` 在一段里出现两次以上,基本就露了。改成句号、逗号或括号。
2. **句子长度整齐**。每句都是 20 到 30 字、每段都是三到四句。真人会有半句话的碎句,也会有
   一句话拖两行。
3. **三项并列**。「A、B 和 C」这种工整的三连排比,LLM 的默认节奏。真人列举经常是两项或者五项,
   而且不对仗。
4. **「真正的重点是:」式揭晓结构**。先铺垫再翻转,读起来像软文。
5. **零不确定、零缺点**。没有一句「这块我还没调好」,没有已知问题,没有具体数字。
6. **形容词是营销词**。satisfying / seamless / genuinely / carefully crafted。
7. **结尾是漂亮的行动号召**。真人经常就是问一个具体问题然后停住。

**改法**:把游戏源码里的**真实数字和真实机制名**塞进去。开发者会说「第三个模型之后开始出
rogue,给你 20 秒」,营销文案只会说「动态事件系统」。下面每一稿都至少带三处这种细节。

**你自己再改一遍会更好**:随手改两三个词、删掉一个你觉得多余的句子、把某处换成你自己的
口气。哪怕只动 10%,机器味就掉一大截。**别一字不动地粘贴三条不同版块。**

## 2️⃣ r/WebGames · 七款合集(与 ① 至少隔 2 天)

**标题**
> Made seven small browser games where the AI part is the actual mechanic

**正文**
> Index with all of them: https://play.agiscorecard.com/ai-games
>
> One rule: no fake AI. If the title says the thing learns, it has to be learning while
> you play, and it has to show you enough that you can beat it on purpose. Turns out that
> is a pretty severe constraint and it wrote most of the designs for me.
>
> Overfit is the clearest example. The boss fits a model to how you moved during the wave,
> then shows you the training report before the next one starts, then aims where that model
> says you'll be. So playing consistently well is the dangerous option and you score most
> when it's confidently wrong.
>
> Mimic gives you a hidden rule that you know and the learner doesn't. You spend labels, it
> classifies 26 items on its own, and if your examples happened to agree on some other
> feature it learns that one instead. End screen tells you which feature it picked up. That
> one annoys people in a good way.
>
> Rest of them: Prompt (write instructions for something that follows the words and not the
> intention), Minima (invisible terrain, you only feel the slope, flat isn't always the
> bottom), Overseer (eight agents, one is off-target, attention runs out before time does),
> Ghostline (time trials against a ghost built from laps you actually finished),
> Singularity Inc (idle AI lab).
>
> All free, all browser, no accounts, no ads. Site also has eleven daily logic puzzles if
> that's more your speed.

## 3️⃣ Show HN(与 ①② 至少隔 3 天;本月若已发过 Show HN 则顺延到下月)

HN 对「我造了个东西」很宽容,对「我推广一个东西」很不宽容。角度必须是约束,不是数量。

**标题**
> Show HN: Browser games where the ML behaviour is the mechanic, not the theme

**正文**
> https://play.agiscorecard.com/ai-games
>
> I kept bouncing off games "about AI" where the AI is in the story and the systems
> underneath are a state machine. So I gave myself one rule for this batch. Whatever the
> title claims the machine does, it has to actually do at runtime, and you have to be able
> to see enough of it to play against it. That's it. Everything else fell out of that.
>
> That rule is what produced the designs rather than the other way around. Overfit shows
> you its training report between waves instead of hiding it, because a faked fit would be
> obvious within one session if you could read it. Mimic has to name the feature it latched
> onto at the end, which is the entire point of the shortcut-learning failure and also the
> thing that makes people replay it. Ghostline's ghost only improves off runs you finished,
> so it tracks you rather than a difficulty curve.
>
> Obvious limitation: these are small hand-written systems, not language models. Nothing
> here calls out to an API. Some of that is just being honest about scale. Most of it is
> that a mechanic you can reason about is more fun to beat than one you have to trust, and
> it means the whole thing works with the wifi off.
>
> No ads, no accounts, no analytics beyond a page counter. They're plain HTML files, you
> can read the source. Built by the same people who run a public AGI prediction scorecard,
> which is where the subject matter comes from.

**准备好被问的两件事**:①「它到底学了什么」,照实答机制;②「是不是又一个 AI 套壳」,
举 Mimic 结算画面当反例,别长篇辩护。
