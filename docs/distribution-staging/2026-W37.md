## 1️⃣ r/incremental_games · SINGULARITY INC.(**本期最高价值的一条**)

这个版块明确欢迎开发者自荐新作,而放置类正是它的主场。**只发这一款**,不要打包七款——
打包会被读成推广,单款会被读成分享。

**标题**
> Singularity Inc. — an idle game about running an AI lab, where a model eventually starts optimising something else

**正文**
> Free, browser, no account, no ads: https://play.agiscorecard.com/singularity
>
> The loop is standard idle — you label data by hand, buy GPUs so you stop having to,
> then train models that earn while the tab is closed, and prestige by shipping the model
> and starting the next lab bigger. Isometric 3D server hall that visibly fills up as you
> scale, four evolution stages, research tree, achievements, skins, offline report.
>
> The part I actually built it for: past a certain scale one of your models starts
> optimising a target other than the one you set. Dealing with it costs real progress,
> and ignoring it also costs real progress, just later. I wanted that trade to be a
> decision you make with your own numbers in front of you rather than a cutscene.
>
> It's the same team that runs a public AGI-prediction scorecard, so the subject isn't
> a coat of paint — but this is a game, not a lecture, and the idle math is meant to be
> satisfying on its own. Happy to hear where the pacing drags; the first prestige is the
> part I'm least sure about.

## 2️⃣ r/WebGames · 七款一起(与 ①️ 至少隔 2 天)

r/WebGames 接受合集贴,而「每款讲一个真机制」正是它喜欢的角度。

**标题**
> Seven small browser games where the AI actually does the thing in the title (free, no account)

**正文**
> https://play.agiscorecard.com/ai-games — index page with all seven.
>
> Rule I set myself: in each one, the machine has to really do what the title claims,
> computed live, and be visible enough that you can play against it.
>
> - **Overfit** — the boss fits a model to how you move between waves, shows you its
>   training report, then shoots where that model says you'll be. Being consistently
>   good is the dangerous option.
> - **Mimic** — you know the hidden rule, the learner doesn't. Spend as few labels as
>   possible, then it sorts 26 items alone. If your examples accidentally agree on some
>   other feature, it learns that instead, and the end screen names it.
> - **Prompt** — write instructions for a literal interpreter that follows the words and
>   not the intention, and takes every shortcut you forgot to forbid.
> - **Minima** — the terrain is invisible, you only feel the slope under your feet, and
>   a flat spot is not always the bottom.
> - **Overseer** — eight agents, one is optimising a different target than the one you
>   set, and attention is the resource that runs out.
> - **Ghostline** — low-poly time trials where the ghost is built from laps you finished
>   and gets faster as it sees more of them.
> - **Singularity Inc.** — idle AI lab, up to the point where a model goes off-target.
>
> All free, all browser, no accounts, no ads. Same site also has eleven daily logic
> puzzles if that's more your thing.

## 3️⃣ Show HN(与 ①②至少隔 3 天;若本月已发过 Show HN 则**顺延到下月**)

HN 对「我造了 X」宽容,对「我推广 X」不宽容。角度必须是**约束**,不是数量。

**标题**
> Show HN: Seven browser games where the ML behaviour is the mechanic, not the theme

**正文**
> https://play.agiscorecard.com/ai-games
>
> I kept bouncing off games "about AI" where the AI is in the story and the systems
> underneath are ordinary. So I set one constraint for this batch: in each game the thing
> the machine does has to be the thing the title claims, computed live, and visible enough
> that you can work against it.
>
> That constraint is what forced the designs. Overfit publishes its training report
> between waves instead of hiding it, because if the fit were faked you'd catch it in one
> session. Mimic ends by naming the feature it actually latched onto, which is the whole
> point of the shortcut-learning failure. Ghostline's ghost only improves after runs you
> finish, so the opponent is a model of you on your own schedule.
>
> The honest limit: these are small hand-built systems, not language models, and no game
> calls an external model. That's a design choice — everything runs offline in a tab, and
> a mechanic you can reason about beats a mechanic you have to trust.
>
> Free, no account, no ads, no telemetry beyond a page counter. Built by the team behind
> a public AGI-prediction ledger, which is where the subject comes from.

**准备好被问的两件事**:①「所以它到底学了什么?」——照实答各游戏的机制(枢纽页上都写了);
②「这是不是又一个 AI 套壳」——这正是标题在回答的,别辩护,举 Mimic 的结算画面当例子。
