   and merchant-side revenue. Keep cause-first and no-purchase branches.

   teaching through primary sources; distinguish observations from self-reports.

   evidence, existing model destinations and existing distribution channels.
5. Ship original content, final HTML/Markdown/metadata and native mobile links;
   retain analytics privacy, full release gates and changed-URL IndexNow receipt.

returned some ECO pages; this is not exhaustive indexing or a ranking report.

automation, keep partial days separate, never prompt repeated clicks, fabricate
