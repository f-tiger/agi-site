# AGI Earn: curated references → a client-delivery plan

2026-09-30. Owner requested a compliant YouTube/TikTok collection within AGI's AI-earning section, followed by deeper optimization, adversarial review and execution. This explicit request authorizes this bounded experiment despite historical autonomous-expansion freezes. No customer demand or revenue has been proven by this release.

## Three prompt improvements

1. Define the user job: an aspiring freelancer with a specific potential buyer must leave with a bounded deliverable, cost assumptions, test and acceptance criteria.
2. Strengthen evidence and commercial boundaries: distinguish source review, workflow testing and audited earnings; official player only; no downloaded media or transcripts; preserve original attribution. Reuse independent AGI membership only for the tools it actually supports.
3. Operationalize: EN/zh routes, homepage discovery, six detailed bilingual briefs, four traceable YouTube references, two explicitly labeled official TikTok written guides, official TikTok/YouTube personal-reference notebook, local planner, export/restore, arithmetic and browser checks, deployment verification. No new domains, cron jobs, database scans or paid AI calls.

## Adversarial pass 1 — demand, retention and revenue (self-review)

- Attack: users can watch free videos on YouTube. Response: page value is a concrete buyer/delivery/acceptance brief, labor-aware quote and exportable plan. Test that value, not video count.
- Attack: income-curious visitors may never deliver work. Response: ask for a real buyer and demand test; do not infer completion from download or a checked box.
- Attack: generic templates already exist. Response: stop at six scoped briefs and one integrated planner; defer paid template-pack promises and mass content production until buyers demonstrate demand.
- Attack: a membership link is not a complete paid workflow. Response: accurately route to existing WorkflowCost/EvidenceBrief cloud workspaces; disclose that the Earn planner itself stays local. Do not rename existing membership or claim its rights cover BPJ.
- Attack: giving authors traffic does not get traffic back. Response: homepage + search discovery and user-initiated canonical sharing are built; creator outreach/public marketing is not performed without an explicit sending request. Referral success remains unknown.

## Adversarial pass 2 — rights, privacy and operational integrity (self-review)

- Attack: public video ≠ all-rights license. Response: source links and official players only; no video binaries in git; never offer download/transcript extraction; policy/correction entry.
- Attack: TikTok creator videos could not be reliably verified from accessible sources. Response: no invented video IDs or fake curation. Two TikTok entries are explicitly written guides; personal notebook supports full public video URLs using the documented official player. Curated TikTok creator inventory remains a gap.
- Attack: an embed can be blocked, deleted or region-restricted. Response: always preserve original links, do not promise playback, and handle official TikTok player errors without mirror fallbacks.
- Attack: a video grid leaks data before choice. Response: original code-drawn cover graphics, no external images, no player until click, no autoplay; source disclosure at load action.
- Attack: untrusted URL/backup may inject code. Response: exact HTTPS host allowlist; no short-link resolving, scraping, server fetching, eval or untrusted innerHTML; bounded local backups and schema checks.
- Attack: a cheap quote hides unpaid labor. Response: cost includes production, review and revisions; target quote accounts for fee and margin denominator; reject invalid or combined rates ≥100%; explicitly exclude taxes/omitted overhead.
- Attack: fake SEO evidence. Response: no fabricated video upload dates, reviews, ratings, earnings or VideoObject eligibility. CollectionPage/WebApplication/BreadcrumbList and canonical/hreflang only; original briefs are crawlable HTML.

## Scope / revenue

Free: official source access, original briefs, local saved cases, up to 20 private video links, planner, Markdown export and JSON backups.
Paid bridge: existing independent AGI membership for WorkflowCost and EvidenceBrief; price/availability remain authoritative on `/members` and `/zh/members`. No new checkout, purchase, wallet mutation or simulated sale. Tool URLs are ordinary links, no affiliate commission configured.
Contribution to this business = attributable settled membership receipts minus refunds and delivery/operating costs. Clicks and exports are not revenue. Automatic source ingestion and unauthorized scraping are excluded.

## Distribution and validation gate

Homepage EN desktop/mobile and CN entry; both AI-tool hubs; EN/zh route pairs; site search; sitemap; llms index; share cards; user-initiated canonical sharing and embeddable planner. No third-party posting performed.

Initial review window: 28 complete days after verified publication. At ≥300 relevant browser page-view events (events, not unique people), look for ≥30 user-initiated planner changes, ≥10 plan exports and ≥5 membership-entry clicks. These are directional operational thresholds, not market benchmarks or user conversion rates. Low traffic means distribution unproven. Visits without actions mean revise positioning; actions without paid demand mean do not expand paid features. Only settled receipt evidence can establish purchases. No new automated task is created by this document.

Use existing first-party event names and distinguish `location=earn_calculate`, `earn_plan_export`, `earn_project_save`, `earn_member`, `earn_player_request`, `earn_share`. Case identifiers only; no quote numbers, user text or personal saved URLs enter analytics. Player request ≠ view/watch completion; share event ≠ acquired visit; export ≠ completed project. DNT/GPC/CI exclusions are inherited from the existing collector. Do not add uncached D1 aggregate endpoints.
