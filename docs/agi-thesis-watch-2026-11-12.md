## Product contract

Existing `/ai-infrastructure` and `/zh/ai-infrastructure`, `#thesis-watch`. Three rules per company:

- Annual revenue growth = (current revenue / positive prior annual revenue − 1) × 100. Annual durations must match within seven days; prior/current year ends must be 330–400 days apart.
- Operating cash-flow margin = operating cash flow / positive annual revenue × 100.
- Cash property capex / positive operating cash flow × 100. Excludes finance leases and acquisitions; ratios over 100% are possible and do not alone establish financial distress.

Strict above/below comparisons, with equality untriggered. The user supplies the condition; sample rules remain labelled examples. Source retrieval older than 72 hours, failed refresh, invalid timestamps, annual end older than 450 days, unsupported/missing facts, or incompatible periods produce an explicit unknown result. The tool evaluates when opened or checked; it does not send personal background alerts. Existing daily deployment attempts source refresh. A changed condition requires a new review checkpoint. Cloud storage remains the existing optional paid value, not a new monitoring entitlement.

Flow: dated SEC snapshot → matching financial facts → user condition → triggered / not triggered / unknown → source review → saved checkpoint → next visit. No broker or model call is involved in condition evaluation. Existing optional AI questions retain their quotas.

## Commercial and growth gates

Free: all source data, checks, local version history and exports. Existing paid membership: cross-device cloud storage, 50 workspaces and ten versions per workspace; payment readiness must be read from the live endpoint, and fixture transfers are not revenue. No paid alert service is offered.

First-party action events, GA4 people/sessions, campaign arrivals, membership intent, paid receipts and renewals are separate measures. Aggregate event data cannot establish user retention. Exclude QA, webdriver, opt-outs, DNT and GPC; no ticker, threshold, note, filenames or source text in telemetry. A repeat check after a saved review is a repeat-work action, not proof of a returning unique person. Reports use complete UTC days and remain unknown on read failure.

Review after 28 complete days. Fewer than 50 qualified landing sessions means acquisition is unresolved; this is an operating gate, not statistical certainty. With adequate arrivals but no own-rule checks, revise the offer or workflow before adding features. Own-rule exports, subsequent reviewed-check actions and requests for cross-device history justify further research; only paid orders and delivered use establish revenue and paid value. Do not extend to quarterly data, personalized alerts or more models without observed demand and a source/cost plan.

Marketing: existing owned YouTube/TikTok brand, campaign `agi-thesis-watch-01`, medium `organic_video`, truthful continuous demonstration and original music. Preserve BPJ/ECO posts, record intent and provider receipts, and do not count queue acceptance as publication. At most one new AGI creative per channel per rolling seven days in the first 28-check pilot. Reuse the existing source-refresh workflow and aggregate endpoint; no additional data crawling cron.
