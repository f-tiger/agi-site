# BPJ conversion paths: value before payment

Owner requests deeper exploration and higher conversion, preserving the registration target and autonomous execution.

Three-pass brief: distinguish registration from purchase; inspect actual entitlements and alternatives rather than infer demand from visits; repair an observed navigation mismatch and leave unproven offers as hypotheses.

## Evidence and decision

On 2026-10-04 the live `/for-vendors` page described quote-first homepage/review arrangements while `/advertise` already supported separately labelled, self-service placements. It lacked a prominent direct route and said pricing required an inquiry. The fix adds a visible, bilingual route to existing reach, pricing and availability before any checkout. Keep the legacy form and its distinct entitlements; no new price, payment method, listing guarantee or order is created. No changes to organic ranking or review criteria. This corrects a journey mismatch, not proof of paid demand.

The live `/members` offer sells cloud project storage/version history, whereas free accounts save followed tools. Bookmark readers are not automatically cloud-project buyers. Raindrop's official [Pro FAQ](https://raindrop.io/pro), read 2026-10-04, offers unlimited bookmarks/collections/devices free and charges for full-text search, permanent copies, backup and maintenance. This is vendor positioning evidence, not independently verified willingness to pay or a reason to copy its product. Feedly pricing could not be read and is excluded from conclusions.

| Audience | Immediate value | Appropriate next action | Payment hypothesis |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tool chooser | Preserve a useful shortlist and its sources | Existing free account/save flow | No proven reason to buy cloud projects merely for bookmarks |
| Repeat workbench user | Reopen project inputs, revisit versions, move devices | Existing cloud-save path after useful work | Existing cloud storage entitlement; no AI quota promise |
| Tool vendor | Reach relevant tool-selection readers | Actual reach and existing self-service placement page | Clearly labelled exposure, never guaranteed sales or editorial endorsement |

## Measurement and execution

Reuse consent-gated GA4 `start_plan` on the public vendor route and existing fixed D1 `/biz/trigger/vendor-sponsor` (first click per page, aggregated with the existing submission-page entry, not an attributable funnel); no form data or new analytics mapping. QA/DNT/GPC/webdriver excluded, QA marker preserved to destination. An entry action is not an inquiry, order, unique person or revenue. Private account pages remain excluded.

Do not read zero complete-day entry events as failed conversion on the launch date: the endpoint excludes today. After at least three complete post-release UTC days, compare same-definition snapshots. Low exposure is insufficient evidence, not rejection. A working decision gate of at least thirty entry actions with no account increase prompts a registration-friction audit; it is not a unique-user conversion rate or statistical result. Daily automation must disclose known owner/self-use accounts and unknown identities. Do not manufacture users or messages.

No card processing, new subscription, discount, buyer outreach or spending is introduced. Payment-method friction is observable but its effect on conversion remains unproven. Keep public traffic/account snapshots out of this repository.

Self-review one: retain free submission and clearly distinguish self-service ads from human review/homepage arrangements; source current prices on the existing destination. Self-review two: preserve QA navigation, no order creation during tests, analytics privacy, original form functionality, mobile usability and SEO/FAQ consistency. Existing CI and IndexNow handle publishing; successful submission does not prove indexing.
