# BPJ ProposalDeck product context

Updated 2026-10-01. New product, no verified paying customers or customer interviews.

Buyer: independent service providers and small client studios that repeatedly revise proposals and hand over editable PowerPoint files. Trigger: an AI draft is useful, but scope, dates, sources and editable delivery still need attention.

Product: browser workbench for client proposals, project reviews and monthly reports. It imports user-supplied structured AI output or Markdown, edits native text/tables/charts, exports editable PPTX and notes, and saves project backups. It does not run a hosted AI model or autonomously contact clients.

Free value: full editing, three styles, examples, PPTX and notes exports, local backup. Paid value: existing BPJ membership offers cloud projects and version history; 9 USDT / 30 days, manually renewed. No new SKU or price change. Checkout readiness must come from the actual member service. API readiness is not a completed payment.

Alternatives: PowerPoint templates; Gamma's free plan including PPTX export; PPT Master in an existing agent environment. We cannot claim uniquely free export, higher conversion, time savings or superior design without evidence. Narrow distinction: a browser handoff for existing AI drafts, client scope, source notes and revision recovery.

Evidence: GitHub attention and first-party category revenue disclosures justify an experiment only. No copied customer claims, revenue estimates, project brand or upstream implementation. PPTX serialization uses pinned MIT PptxGenJS with bundled dependency notices.

Distribution: existing homepage studio entry, studio directory, site search, discover/MCP feature map, guide and three explicit fictional examples. No ads, unsolicited outreach or scheduled external posts.

Funnel: own-project export, member page open, cloud handoff, acknowledged save, and separately verified paid orders. Demo, QA and browser sessions are not people or revenue. Existing first-party analytics receives event names only, not client content. A member click is intent, not payment.

Decision threshold (hypothesis): observe at least 20 distinct opted-in, qualified project sessions or direct user confirmations before deciding whether to invest in model hosting. Look for at least five repeated revision workflows and two independently verified purchases attributable to this product. Current aggregate events cannot prove distinct people or qualification; do not infer those from counters. If free exports occur without repeat editing/cloud demand, improve handoff or stop the subscription expansion. These are future gates, not measured outcomes.

Voice: concrete, calm and bilingual. Sell the deliverable. Describe AI/manual boundaries and cloud upload before the action. Do not promise sales, jobs, investment returns or autonomous income.
