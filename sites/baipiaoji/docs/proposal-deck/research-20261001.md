# GitHub-first commercialization decision, 2026-10-01

## Three refinements before execution

1. Correct scope: investigate other commercialization directions, rather than iterate ReleaseCheck again.
2. Evidence: discover high-star AI repositories, cross-check official weekly Trending, fetch exact repository metadata, and separate licenses, product pricing and revenue claims.
3. Delivery: choose one distinct product with an existing distribution/payment path, implement a useful free deliverable and real paid capability, then verify deployment and measurement. No new spending or invented demand.

## Adversarial review 1: why would someone choose this?

Gamma already has free PPTX export; PPT Master already supports editable output. A generic prompt-to-PPT clone is weak. Narrow the job to client handoff: agreed scope, fees, schedule, source notes and revisions. Reuse the user's existing assistant instead of inventing another model subscription. The product must create a real editable file, not screenshots or a decorative demo. Differentiation and willingness to pay remain hypotheses.

## Adversarial review 2: where could the business claim fail?

Cloud history is useful only for repeat work. A one-off user may never need it. Therefore keep all exports free and charge only through an existing working membership entitlement, with no new payment claims. Track actual actions, keep fictional examples separate, and do not call bot or QA activity customers. Hosting a model is deferred until paid evidence and reliable quota exist. This release is an AI-workflow delivery product, not an autonomous hosted agent.

## Implementation and distribution

Original browser editor and layouts; pinned PptxGenJS 4.0.1 for native Office Open XML output. Text, tables, charts and notes remain editable. Long body/table content paginates instead of disappearing. Import validates size, schema, numbers and bounds; previews escape input. Same-origin cloud handoff checks sender, origin, nonce and current snapshot. No API secrets or client text in telemetry.

Design: white presentation paper, navy text #142D4E, blue action #185BCC, slate secondary #53657A, quiet workbench #E9EDF3. System Chinese sans-serif/Arial for compatibility. The slide itself is the dominant visual; the editor uses a narrow project rail and a large landscape preview. Rejected a generic marketing card wall in favor of showing the actual deliverable. Two alternate restrained themes support client work without decorative imagery.

Distribution ships through existing BPJ homepage, studio, search and discovery map, plus a bilingual guide with three fictional examples. The guide links to the original open-source inspiration and honest free alternatives. No third-party messages, ad purchases or scheduled social posts were made.
