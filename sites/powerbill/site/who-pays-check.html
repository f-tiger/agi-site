Which rules decide your exposure?

Whether a new data centre can push cost onto your bill is decided by a handful of mechanisms, and which ones apply depends on where you live and how you buy power. Answer eight questions and this names them, and tells you which filing to go and read.

What this is: an editorial mapping from your answers to the mechanisms described on who decides who pays. It is not legal or regulatory advice, and it does not know your utility. Its job is to make your next search a specific one instead of a vague one. Nothing is uploaded.
