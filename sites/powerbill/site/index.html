The AGI era · the bill side of the build-out

Your bill went up.
Was it the data centres?

Sometimes, in some places — and almost never the way the headlines say. Data centre demand reaches households through capacity and grid costs, not through the price of electricity itself. There is also serious evidence pointing the other way, which most pages quietly drop. This site keeps both, with dates and sources, and gives you two tools that use only the numbers you type.

Split my bill: rate vs usage Which rules apply where I live The evidence, both directions
The one-line version: a record capacity auction can reach your bill without your utility doing anything; a large steady customer can also spread fixed network costs and push average prices down. Which happens to you is set by rules, in proceedings that take public comment.
