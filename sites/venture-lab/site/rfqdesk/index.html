New to CSV? See a filled example

A CSV is a plain-text table. Each line is one supplier. Keep the first line as column names and enter costs in one currency.

supplier,unit_price,moq,pack_size,freight,setup,lead_days
Your supplier,2.45,200,50,90,0,14

MOQ is the minimum quantity you must buy. Pack size is the order increment.

All prices in the selected currency. Blank costs are unknown and will be rejected; enter 0 only if confirmed. Use one product with equal specifications.

Before you begin.

Is this a supplier marketplace?

No. Bring your own quotes. We do not verify suppliers, contact them, place orders or arrange payment.

Which quote is cheapest?

The table sorts known cash outlay for the calculated order quantity. Extra stock, quality, terms and missing costs can change the actual decision.

Does this calculate customs duty?

No. All quotes must use the same currency, equivalent specifications and consistent freight scope. Tax, duty and unquoted charges are excluded.
