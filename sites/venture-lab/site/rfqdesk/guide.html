PRACTICAL GUIDE

Compare supplier quotes when MOQs differ

Start with the same buying question

A unit price is not a complete quote. Compare one product, equivalent specifications, one currency and freight to the same location. If a supplier has not quoted a fee, ask first; a blank is not zero.

The calculation

order units = ceil(max(required units, MOQ) / pack size) × pack size
known cost = order units × unit price + freight + setup
unit cost = known cost / order units

For 300 required units, a quote at $2.10 with MOQ 500 and pack size 100 requires 500 units. With $250 freight and $50 setup, known cash outlay is $1,350. Another quote at $2.45 with MOQ 200, pack size 50, $90 freight and no setup requires 300 units and $825. The lower unit price needs more cash and leaves 200 extra units.

Ask for the missing terms

Record quote validity, production and shipping time separately, quality requirements, payment schedule and the named place for the agreed shipping terms. The RFQ export prompts these questions; it does not send them or create a supplier agreement.

What this comparison excludes

Tax, duty, insurance, inspection and other unquoted costs are excluded. Currency conversion is not provided. This is arithmetic from your inputs, not a customs estimate or supplier recommendation. Do not compare quotes with incompatible freight scope.

Beyond the spreadsheet

The useful next step is a repeatable quote round and approval history. A marketplace would additionally require real suppliers, verified buyer demand and an agreed transaction process. None of those is implied by this calculator.
