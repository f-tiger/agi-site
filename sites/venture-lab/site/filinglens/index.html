WHY PERIODS MATTER

A quarter is not
a year to date.

“2025 Q3” can describe multiple facts. Revenue for July–September and revenue for January–September are different measurements. FilingLens matches the exact start date, end date and unit before comparing anything.

It also keeps same-day conflicting values unresolved. A tidy answer is only useful when the data supports it.
