Before you begin.

Do you connect to my provider?

No. The current tool accepts the documented normalized CSV format. It never requests API keys, sends prompts or changes routing.

Are these live model prices?

No. Totals use the billed_usd values you supply. There is no claim that a cheaper model will provide equivalent quality.

What does the projection mean?

Spend divided by complete days represented, multiplied by days in the month. It assumes an unchanged pace and excludes any costs missing from your data.
