Two symbols, two rules. FAQ

What are the rules of a binary puzzle?

Fill the grid with two symbols so that each row and column contains an equal number of both, and no three identical symbols are adjacent horizontally or vertically. Some versions also forbid duplicate rows; this site's boards are uniquely solvable from the two core rules and the givens.

Is this the same as Tango or Binairo?

Same family. Binairo/Takuzu use 0s and 1s, LinkedIn's Tango uses suns and moons with extra = and × edge hints. The two core constraints — balanced counts and no three in a row — are shared across all of them.

What is the fastest deduction in binary puzzles?

Cap every adjacent pair: two identical symbols in a row force the opposite symbol on both ends. Together with filling the middle of an X_X gap, these two moves solve most of a 6×6 before counting is even needed.
