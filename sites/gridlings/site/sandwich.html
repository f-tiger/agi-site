How to play

  • Tap a cell to cycle through the numbers (1–n) and back to empty.
  • Every row and column contains each number exactly once.
  • Each outside clue is the sum of the numbers between 1 and n (the largest number) in that row or column — the "sandwich filling".
  • Every board has exactly one solution, machine-verified before publication.

The sandwich-sudoku idea, distilled to a five-minute daily grid. Sister puzzles: Gridlings.
