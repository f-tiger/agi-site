How to play

  • Tap a cell to cycle: empty → ★ → × (a note that no star goes there) → empty.
  • Place stars so every row, every column and every colored region ends with exactly the star count shown (2★ on the daily 10×10; 1★ on the easy 8×8).
  • No two stars may touch — not even diagonally.
  • Every board has exactly one solution, machine-verified before publication. You never have to guess.

If you enjoy LinkedIn's Queens or Netflix's daily star puzzle, this is the full-strength version of that habit: the two-star format on a 10×10 board that the one-star clones don't offer, with unlimited practice and an archive of dailies. Sister puzzles: Gridlings.
