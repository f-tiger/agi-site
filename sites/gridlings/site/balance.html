How to play

  • Tap a cell to cycle: empty → ☀️ → 🌙 → empty.
  • Every row and column ends with exactly three suns and three moons.
  • Never three of the same symbol in a row — horizontally or vertically.

FAQ

How do you play Balance?

Fill every cell with a sun or a moon so each row and column holds exactly three of each, and no three identical symbols sit adjacent horizontally or vertically. Tap a cell to cycle sun, moon, empty.

Is there a new Balance puzzle every day?

Yes — a new daily board goes live at 00:00 UTC, the same board for every player worldwide, with streak tracking. Unlimited free play in three difficulties and a full archive of past dailies are also free, with no account and no ads.

Can Balance be solved without guessing?

Yes. Every published board is machine-verified before release: a solver proves exactly one solution exists and is reachable by logical deduction, so there is always a provable next step.
