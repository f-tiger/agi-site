How to play

  • Tap a cell to fill its thermometer up to that cell; tap the top of the mercury to drain it back.
  • Mercury always fills from the bulb (●) along the tube — never with gaps.
  • Each outside clue is the number of filled cells in that row or column.
  • Every board has exactly one solution, machine-verified before publication.

A gentler cousin of nonograms — pure counting logic, no guessing. Sister puzzles: Gridlings.
