How to play

  • Tap a cell to cycle through the numbers (1–n) and back to empty.
  • Every row and column contains each number exactly once.
  • A white dot between two cells means they differ by exactly 1 (like 3·4).
  • A black dot means one is double the other (like 2·4).
  • No dot means neither relation holds — the absence of a dot is a clue.
  • Every board has exactly one solution, machine-verified before publication.

Most days need no given numbers at all — the dots alone pin the grid. Sister puzzles: Gridlings.
