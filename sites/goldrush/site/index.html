How the site evolves — the algorithm, stated honestly

This site is maintained by AI sessions running a published editorial loop we call Evolution Protocol v0.1 — a tree-search-shaped process: reader clicks and citations score each entry; winners are expanded into deeper pages; entries nobody touches for 90 days are pruned to an archive; at most one new candidate claim is added per cycle, and only after passing three gates (first-party data, real demand, a named reader value). Every step is a public git commit. It is an auditable algorithm, not a neural network — a site that grades inflated AI claims does not get to make one about itself.

Governance — who controls this, in writing

One human owner holds absolute control: every deploy is a public commit; a one-file kill switch freezes the pipeline instantly; the AI sessions that maintain the site cannot spend, transfer, or invest anything. Three permanent commitments: no token, ever · no payment ever changes a verdict · misses stay on the ledger.
