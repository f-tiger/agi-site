Claim Grader

Grades money claims per the Claim Ledger Protocol v0.1 — and reads any site's /claimledger.json. This is one self-contained file: save it (Ctrl/Cmd-S), rename it, pre-fill it, make it yours — the protocol blesses adapted copies. Works offline.
e.g. "Our AI agent earns users 3% a week" · "This course pays for itself in a month"
Describe the public evidence, not the person. "No audited evidence as of today" beats "scam".
Be specific enough that the seller could actually satisfy it. A verdict without a flip condition is an opinion.
