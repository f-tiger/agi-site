# EcoBack household tools — 2026-09-25

Scope: ECO household decisions. The root portfolio positioning and separate B2B evidence experiments retain their own audiences.

Audience: German, French, Spanish and Italian households trying to understand electricity bills and compare offers. English is an access language, not a UK billing model.

Job: “Will switching really save me money after the bonus ends, and does my usage fit this tariff?”

Positioning: a free, inspectable calculation using the visitor’s own figures. Save locally, export and share a reproducible comparison. Existing device running-cost and storage tools are complementary.

Alternatives: CHECK24 in Germany; the official Energie-Info, CNMC and ARERA comparison services. Those services already cover supplier discovery. ECO’s initial gap to test is transparent first/recurring-year costs, scenario retention and flexible household inputs. This is a hypothesis, not proven demand for ECO.

Promise: explain arithmetic and assumptions. No live cheapest-provider ranking, auto-switching or guaranteed savings. Sample prices are always marked. No checkout for this tool.

Primary action: complete a comparison with own inputs. Retention actions: save, export, share. Measure calculation and deliberate sharing separately from sample runs and QA traffic, filtering the five new page paths. Local saving and export are not measured in this release. Anonymous events are not unique people, completed switches, verified bills or revenue.
