Before you replace a filter

How do I find my exact air purifier model?

Read the model name on the rating label on the back or underside of your air purifier. Keep every letter, number and suffix. Select that exact model here; a similar-looking name does not establish a match.

Does this finder cover every replacement filter?

No. This first US directory covers only the Levoit models listed in the table. An unlisted model means we have not documented it here, not that no replacement filter exists.

What does a source check establish?

We check the manufacturer listing for the stated model-to-filter relationship. This is a documentation check, not a physical fit test, independent performance test or guarantee of seller authenticity.

Why is an Amazon search sometimes unavailable?

Amazon search links appear only after you select an exact model, when the source record is verified and its check is no more than 30 days old. A source conflict, failed check or older record keeps the official evidence visible but disables the Amazon link.

Does an Amazon search identify one guaranteed product?

No. Amazon results may include third-party filters and other variants. Compare the manufacturer filter name, exact purifier model and seller listing before buying. We do not show current price or stock and do not claim that search results are original filters.

Where are my search and saved models stored?

Model filtering happens in your browser. Saved models stay in this browser only and are not synchronized to an account. Search text and selected model names are not sent to analytics; fixed tool-action and affiliate-click measurement follows the site privacy settings.
