## Country and language are different choices

This English overview serves readers across countries. Germany, France, Spain and Italy have separate local-language pages with national resources. We do not infer your tariff, permissions or eligibility from your browser language or location.

## Start with the job the device must do

A fan is not equivalent to an air conditioner. A dehumidifier does not repair a leak. Before comparing running costs, check capacity, room temperature, noise, drainage and installation. Confirm equal service only when both options meet the same need.

## Use bills and installation quotes

Enter variable electricity charges including relevant taxes. For time-of-use tariffs, use a consumption-weighted price over the actual operating hours. Include any changed fixed charges in annual additional costs. Do not count unchanged standing charges as savings.

## Local decisions need local evidence

German heating support has a KfW eligibility process. France has the Médiateur national de l’énergie tariff comparator. Spain provides CNMC tariff comparison and IDAE self-consumption guidance. Italy has Portale Offerte and ENEA tax-relief guidance. A tax deduction is not an upfront cash grant.

## How the calculation works

Cooling/drying: W ÷ 1000 × hours × days. Heating: useful heat ÷ seasonal ratio. Saving = annual cost A − annual cost B. Payback = max(0, cost B − cash grant − cost A) ÷ positive annual saving.

Solar: avoided imports = generation × self-use share. Add effective surplus credit and subtract annual costs. A is no PV. Energy B is generation, not consumption; negative running cost is a net benefit. No storage dispatch simulation.

No financing, discounting, inflation, equipment replacement, degradation or delayed tax credits. Results do not establish capacity, installation permission or grant eligibility.
