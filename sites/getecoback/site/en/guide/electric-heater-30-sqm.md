## What does heating 30 m² cost?

At full power, 2,100 W draws about **€0.63 per hour** (calculated at €0.30/kWh — put in your own tariff). For a season: with 5 heating hours a day, a thermostat duty cycle of about 40 % and 150 heating days that is roughly **€189 per season**. The formula is transparent: watts ÷ 1000 × price × hours × duty cycle × days. Electric resistance heating turns one kilowatt-hour of electricity into exactly one kilowatt-hour of heat — that physics is why a heat pump beats it for whole-home, all-winter heating.
