The same 12L model lists 12.18 L/day at 30°C / 80% RH. That does not mean it will remove 12 litres per day in a cool UK bedroom. Both models extract less at the cooler 10°C / 60% test point than at 20°C / 60%.

Does the lower-wattage machine cost less to finish the job?

Not necessarily. At the 20°C / 60% test point, the 12L model has lower power but also lower water extraction. Hourly cost is not the same as cost to reach a target humidity or dry a load. Real conditions change during operation, and cycling, ventilation and new moisture affect the result. Measure a whole comparable session before claiming a saving.

When a larger capacity may be useful

Consider a larger model if the smaller one cannot maintain the chosen conditions despite correct use and resolving moisture sources, or if its tank and operating schedule do not suit you. This table alone cannot establish the capacity required for a specific home. A larger tank may reduce emptying, but tank size is not extraction capacity.

Why bedroom count is an incomplete shortcut

Note the actual room volume, temperature, moisture source, door arrangement and airflow. A unit in a hallway is not guaranteed to control closed bedrooms. Noise at your chosen setting, drainage, placement clearances and the ability to move the unit also matter. Ask for performance at relevant temperature and humidity, rather than relying solely on the advertised number of rooms.

A cold room changes the decision

Check the manufacturer’s operating range and performance curve. A cold-room decision may involve compressor versus desiccant operation, but do not assume that a technology label proves the lower whole-session cost. Follow the actual manual and account for the room’s moisture source.

Model match and a no-purchase option

The official Meaco 10L/12L H13 filter page, read on 17 October 2026, lists Arete One 10L, Arete One 12L, Arete Two 10L and Arete Two 12L. This is not verification for 20L, 25L, Platinum or a similar-looking filter.

For this verified family, Meaco says the additional HEPA filter does not have to be fitted all the time. For dehumidifying alone it is optional. Keep and maintain the standard dust filter as directed in your model manual. The manufacturer says adding HEPA slightly reduces water extraction efficiency and increases noise. This page does not assess medical needs or promise health benefits.

Do not wash, cut or improvise a HEPA filter. The standard dust filter and the replaceable HEPA filter are different parts; follow the exact model manual for cleaning and replacement.

UK purchase checklist

Check the exact model and seller before paying. A German listing or an English-language page does not establish UK compatibility or delivery. EcoBack has not verified a UK affiliate buying link for these models; the manufacturer links below are ordinary sources, not commission links.

  • Confirm the exact model generation and condition-matched extraction and watts.
  • Check noise at the intended setting, tank access, drainage and placement.
  • Identify leaks or ventilation faults that a purchase would not fix.
  • Use your own tariff and a full session, not only maximum nameplate watts.
  • Confirm the model generation, UK plug and rated voltage in the manufacturer specification and the exact seller listing; do not assume an imported variant is equivalent.
  • Check delivery to your postcode, including any Northern Ireland or island exclusions, total delivered price in GBP, warranty coverage and who handles returns.
  • Check current availability and return terms directly; this page makes no stock or price promise.
