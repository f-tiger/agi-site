## Free tricks (do these first)

- **Close blinds and windows during the day.** Keep the hot afternoon air and sun out; only open up once it's cooler outside than in (usually after midnight).

- **Create a cross-breeze at night.** Open windows on opposite sides of the flat to pull cooler night air through.

- **Cool your pulse points.** A cold, damp cloth on wrists, neck and ankles lowers how hot you feel fast.

- **Take a lukewarm shower before bed** — not cold, which makes your body generate heat afterward.

- **Ditch the duvet; use a thin cotton sheet** and swap synthetic bedding for breathable cotton or linen.

- **Freeze a water bottle** and place it by your feet, or a damp towel in the freezer for 20 minutes.

- **Sleep low.** Heat rises — a ground-floor room or even a mattress on the floor is cooler.
