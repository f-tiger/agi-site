The base is about **340 BTU per m²**, adjusted for ceiling height, sun exposure, people (each person beyond two adds heat) and heat sources such as an open kitchen. It's a proven rule of thumb — the exact figure also depends on insulation and windows. ## Frequently asked questions

**How many BTU do I need for my room?**As a rule of thumb, about 340 BTU per square metre. Rough guide: up to 15 m² needs 7,000–9,000 BTU, 15–25 m² needs 9,000–12,000 BTU, 25–35 m² needs 12,000–14,000 BTU, and over 35 m² needs 14,000+ BTU. Add capacity for high ceilings, strong sun, several people or an open kitchen.

**What happens if the air conditioner has too few BTU?**An underpowered unit runs at full load constantly, never reaches the target temperature and still uses a lot of power. Size to your room or slightly above.

**Is more BTU always better?**No. An oversized unit short-cycles on and off, cools unevenly, is louder and costs more to buy. Matching the room is best.
