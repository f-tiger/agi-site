## How many litres per day for 30 m²?

For 30 m² plan on 20–25 litres/day, more in very damp rooms. Size up for basements, ground floors, lots of indoor laundry drying, or after water damage. The manufacturer's litres/day figure is measured under warm, very humid lab conditions — real-world extraction is lower, which is another reason not to undersize.
