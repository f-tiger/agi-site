Read extraction and room temperature together

The captured sources do not provide an unambiguous daily extraction figure with its test conditions. No litres figure is invented or inferred from the model name here. We do not turn laboratory litres per day into a promise for eight hours or a laundry-drying time. Compare products at matching temperature and relative humidity wherever possible; floor area alone is insufficient.

Captured operating range: 5 °C – 35 °C. Compare it with the actual temperature where the unit will stand. Permission to operate within a range does not mean constant extraction throughout that range.

Tank or drain: plan the work before buying

The captured tank specification is 2.5 L. This describes container size, not the time between emptying. Tank volume divided by laboratory extraction is not a reliable emptying interval: humidity, temperature, target setting and the cut-off level are unknown. Start with regular checks and observe the actual fill level.

Continuous drainage is not positively confirmed in the captured information. If emptying a tank is impractical, verify the manual and connection before relying on a hose or pump.A full-tank shut-off is not sufficiently documented here; verify it in the manual before use.

Compare noise figures fairly

The manufacturer lists 35 and 38 dB(A) (Manufacturer operating modes; measurement distance not stated in this table). Measurement distance, operating mode and fan speed are part of the comparison. If they are absent, the number cannot support a like-for-like comparison with differently measured products. Sound at a bed or desk also depends on distance, the room and vibration. Check the return terms before buying; a decibel figure does not guarantee undisturbed sleep.

Three conditions for a shortlist

  1. The intended room is within the documented operating range (5 °C – 35 °C).
  2. You can check and empty the tank (2.5 L), or have verified the exact drain arrangement.
  3. You accept the illustrative £0.42 a day at 8 h and £0.35/kWh, and will check real consumption with a suitable plug-in energy monitor.

When to pause the purchase

  • Do not choose a unit for a cold room solely from its model number or a warm laboratory extraction figure.
  • Do not plan unattended running until drainage, shut-off and the manual’s conditions are clear.
  • Do not buy solely on a guaranteed saving or bedroom noise promise: neither is established by this page.

A UK plug and delivery eligibility are not verified here. An English page or GBP calculation does not establish either; confirm the exact UK variant, mains connection and delivery before ordering.
