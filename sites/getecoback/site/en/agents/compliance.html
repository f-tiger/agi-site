Sources and limits

The diagnosis links to primary sources. Applicability depends on the product, market, economic operator and current rules.

  • European Commission: CE marking
  • European Commission: CBAM
  • European Commission: New Legislative Framework

This is a planning tool. Low-confidence or safety-critical products require qualified professional review.

Questions

Is this CE certification?

No. The page collects evidence and marks gaps. It does not issue certification or a binding legal or conformity assessment.

What happens to my files?

This MVP does not accept files. Selection values stay in the browser. A paid pilot needs separate protected upload and deletion rules.

How does this become a paid product?

The first paid deliverable is a reviewed evidence and sales pack for one product family. Update monitoring and team access can support recurring revenue later.
