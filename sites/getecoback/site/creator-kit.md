# EcoBack Creator Kit

Canonical (HTML): https://getecoback.com/creator-kit.html

EcoBack Creator Kit provides country calculators, UTM-tagged links, iframe embed code, YouTube copy and a forum answer template. Share only in relevant communities and follow each community's rules.

- Germany: https://getecoback.com/rechner.html
- France: https://getecoback.com/fr/calculateur.html
- Spain: https://getecoback.com/es/calculadora.html
- Italy: https://getecoback.com/it/calcolatore.html
- Embeddable widgets: https://getecoback.com/widgets.html

The calculators are estimates, not quotes. Inputs and formulas remain visible, and grants must be verified with the linked authority.
