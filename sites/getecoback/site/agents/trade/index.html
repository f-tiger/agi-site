Evidence boundary

TradeCheck can find identifier, currency, line mapping, price, quantity, duplicate-reference and arithmetic issues. It cannot verify delivery, quality, customs, sanctions, bank details or payment eligibility. Empty exceptions never mean “safe to pay”.
