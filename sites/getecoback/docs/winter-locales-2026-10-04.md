# Winter decisions in English and Italian — 2026-10-04

The owner requested faster multilingual expansion. This bounded release adds seven English and seven Italian winter decision guides, one task hub per language and six localized calculator experiences using the existing three calculation functions. This explicitly requested batch is separate from the daily automation's one-new-page allowance; no new schedule or expense is introduced.

The eight German source pages, eight English pages and eight Italian pages form reciprocal language groups. English and Italian homepages link both their hub and all seven guides. Calculators appear on the existing tools directory. Canonicals, FAQ markup, search, sitemap, llms inventory, Markdown mirrors and opt-in analytics use the existing build pipeline. The final localization pass runs after country/category generators so the entry links survive a deployment.

## Editorial boundaries

- Technical evidence comes from the German winter source guides and their named consumer-advice sources, originally checked on 2026-10-04. Translations/adaptations make source language and jurisdiction limits explicit.
- This is an extension of existing language assets, not evidence of new search demand or Italian market validation. No claim of keyword volume, current rankings, indexing, sales or guaranteed savings.
- Water heating, radiator controls, ventilation and product compatibility are described with their actual limits. German rental law is not presented as local law elsewhere.
- New localized guides have no new marketplace enrollment or product-test claims. Existing site shopping routes remain available through established navigation.
- EUR, GBP, USD and CHF are display/accounting choices on the new calculators, not exchange-rate conversions or current tariff defaults. Litres, Celsius and kWh are explicit.
- Formula modules are shared with German calculators. Form labels, results, zero/negative outcomes, errors and exported text are localized. Inputs stay local; the existing consent-gated fixed event contract is unchanged. Example runs never count as completed personal calculations.

## Validation and measurement

The release gate checks 24 routes, eight reciprocal language groups, all 14 localized FAQ pages, home entries, local links, sitemap, search and Markdown. Browser coverage exercises nine calculator pages across three languages, example versus confirmed use, duplicate completion suppression, export, currency changes, invalidation, consent withdrawal and mobile/desktop layouts. No live analytics events are sent by tests. GA4 backend receipt still requires genuine traffic and account-side evidence.

Review after 28 days. Inspect qualified visits, actual confirmed tool completions and onward journeys by language and page; do not infer revenue from clicks. A practical continuation gate is at least 100 qualified visits per language plus 10 genuine tool completions for that language. Lower reach is an acquisition/discovery gap, not proof of absent demand. Merchant-confirmed revenue is a separate measure. These operating thresholds are not statistical significance claims and do not create a recurring job.
