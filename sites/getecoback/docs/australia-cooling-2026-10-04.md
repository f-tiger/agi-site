# Australian cooling expansion — 2026-10-04

The owner requested Australian expansion after the multilingual winter release. Australia already has a home-comfort hub and window-kit checklist. This bounded release adds five local cooling decisions (portable AC cost, fan cost, evaporative versus refrigerated cooling, rental shade, bedroom night cooling), two AUD cost calculators and four entry blocks. It is an explicitly requested batch, not a change to the daily one-page automation limit.

## Evidence and decisions

Read on 2026-10-04:
- [Energy Made Easy — tariff types](https://www.energymadeeasy.gov.au/article/electricity-tariffs): single rate, time-of-use, controlled load and demand pricing. Calculators use the reader’s cents/kWh and exclude supply and demand charges.
- [CHOICE — portable AC guide](https://www.choice.com.au/home-and-living/cooling/air-conditioners/buying-guides/portable-air-conditioners): heat exhaust, installation constraints and limitations of single-duct units.
- [CHOICE — evaporative versus refrigerated cooling](https://www.choice.com.au/home-and-living/cooling/portable-air-conditioners/articles/portable-evaporative-coolers-vs-portable-air-conditioners): humidity and ventilation limitations. We do not copy its product rankings or claim appliance tests.
- [Healthdirect — hot weather](https://www.healthdirect.gov.au/hot-weather-risks-and-staying-cool): shade, a cooler environment and escalation for heat illness. Cost calculations cannot assess safe indoor conditions.

The existing energy.gov.au heating/cooling and northern climate links, plus YourHome passive cooling/shading pages, returned HTTP 503 during this session. They are not described as freshly verified sources. No keyword-volume, current ranking, new traffic or revenue evidence was available for this batch; these are evidence-informed use cases, not proven popular searches. October is not treated as winter across Australia, and humid coastal conditions are not equated with dry inland conditions.
