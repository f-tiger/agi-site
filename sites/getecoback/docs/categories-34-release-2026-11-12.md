# ECO categories 3–4: robot vacuums and humidifiers

The owner explicitly requested completion of categories 3 and 4 after the first release shipped only thermostats and wet/dry floor cleaners. This instruction supersedes the research brief's conditional robot deferral and the September seasonal no-retry verdict. It does not establish demand, traffic or commission growth.

## Product scope and acceptance

- Category 3 uses `/guide/saugroboter-kaufberatung.html`: Dreame D10 Plus Gen 2 (manual mop care), Dreame L10s Ultra Gen 2 (mop-washing tank station) and Roborock Saros 10R (low chassis and bounded double-threshold claim). Housing constraints come before model choice; exact generation, floor/mop limitations, station placement and user-performed maintenance remain visible. Manufacturer single/double-threshold claims are not universal apartment compatibility guarantees. C10 was excluded after its German storefront and 100 V manual could not establish the exact regional electrical variant; the D10 replacement has a matching 220–240 V German manual.
- Category 4 deepens the existing `/guide/luftbefeuchter-ratgeber.html`, keeping its canonical rather than adding a competing guide. Stadler Form Oskar O-020, Beurer LB 200 and Beurer LB 55 expose controls, water, cleaning, replacement parts and power limits. Oskar's exact white SKU is supported by the manufacturer's product overview rather than a color-selecting web URL alone. Measure humidity and consider keeping existing equipment before choosing a technology. Steam is not a medical or hygiene upgrade claim. Related electricity and white-dust guides retain their separate tasks with corrected, bounded language.
- Official evidence, accurate model searches and source dates control eligibility. Unverified, future-dated or older-than-90-day catalogs suppress merchant links. Search links are labelled as searches, not locked product pages. No guessed prices, availability, ASINs, scores or own device tests.
- Browser acceptance checks expected eligible queries from the dated evidence, not a permanent count of three links. This allows an intended stale-link withdrawal to deploy while still rejecting missing eligible links, extra links and incorrect queries. Date-boundary fixtures exercise future, day 90, day 91 and unresolved evidence.
- Shared discovery connects all four categories from existing household entry points. `/rechner.html#home-fit-robot` opens and resets the existing robot check as an example; it does not claim to certify one of the models. Generic cooling/dehumidifier promotions are excluded from the curated buying pages and humidifier support guides.
- Static HTML and commerce-free Markdown retain the same facts and official citations. Search, sitemap, social previews, final GA4 coverage, mobile/no-JS review and production checks are release gates. Additive `buying_paths.robot` and `buying_paths.humidifier` measure exact pageview and merchant-click events without changing earlier cohorts or inventing users/orders.

## Competitor mechanisms applied

The implementation review read [home&smart's low-profile robot guide](https://www.homeandsmart.de/flache-saugroboter-test-ueberraschender-testsieger-0067-100542), [CHIP's robot-with-mopping guide](https://www.chip.de/test-kaufberatung/haushalt-garten/saug-wisch-roboter-im-test-die-besten-modelle_44f0027e-0d9b-4732-aab9-605e413df9ca.html), [home&smart's humidifier guide](https://www.homeandsmart.de/die-besten-luftbefeuchter-test-vergleich-2026-100348) and [CHIP's humidifier guide](https://www.chip.de/artikel/Luftbefeuchter-Test-Alle-Luftbefeuchter-im-Vergleich_128572169.html) on 2026-11-12. Their layouts informed four concrete mechanisms: physical fit before shortlist; mopping capability separate from station care; technology/control/maintenance roles; and ongoing water, energy and consumable costs before shopping. Product facts are verified separately against official sources. No competitor scores, photographs, prices, conversion rates or medical claims are imported.
