# ECO: different categories, concrete products and useful tools

Owner steering on 2026-11-13 explicitly corrects the earlier traffic emphasis:
expand actual products in different categories, and provide tools suited to the
categories. This batch delivers 48 model records in 12 categories in a single
German catalog, `/produkte.html`, alongside the existing specialist guides.
It does not claim that a 100-fold inventory expansion, click goal or revenue has
been achieved. Repeated links, color variants, generated pages and unreviewed
candidates are not additional distinct products.

## Scope and original value

`data/product-catalog.json` is the source of truth. Each record has a category,
brand, exact model/package, intended role, bounded facts, limits, a dated official
German source and a precise merchant search. Twelve categories each contain four
models. This is an editorial selection with manufacturer evidence, not a lab test,
marketplace inventory, popularity ranking or a recommendation for every home.
The existing dehumidifier, thermostat, robot, humidifier and wet-floor-cleaner
catalogs retain their own deeper evidence and pages.

Every category exposes a practical calculation/check or a precise existing tool:
air-volume planning; fan electricity costs; paired hygrometer readings; steam
surface permissions; glass area; wet/dry material suitability; vacuum consumable
costs; heater electricity; laundry drying costs; kettle capacity; two-zone cooking
scheduling; and grill placement measurements. Calculations use reader-supplied
assumptions and labelled examples, not inferred product test results. A planning
result does not certify CADR suitability, cleanliness, material safety, cooking
doneness, installation compatibility or savings. Local inputs are not uploaded.
Existing useful tools are reused instead of building duplicates.

The buyer is a household comparing a concrete appliance and its operating needs.
Free value is the sourced selection and category-specific check, usable without
an account. Revenue continues through disclosed eligible Amazon.de purchases;
site clicks, orders, refunds, commission and settlement remain separate.

## Evidence and conservative commerce

All 48 exact German product sources were opened during the research batch.
Four research groups supplied separate category records; a further reviewer
reopened seven cross-group sources and checked identity/duplication. That sample
is not a claim of a second full independent audit or hands-on device testing.
Failed or generic redirects were excluded. AF180 dimensions, EY5058 capacity and
KG2395 plug-table ambiguities are not used as facts; the recorded core identity
and quoted functions were checked separately. No manufacturer price, stock,
score, savings percentage, third-party photograph or medical benefit is borrowed.

The validator rejects malformed/duplicate models and queries, unknown categories,
unapproved citation destinations and the permanently excluded energy-storage or
balcony-PV identities. It uses the existing storage veto on category and product
identity/search, rather than misclassifying a hygrometer's Max-Min memory as
energy storage. Citation approval accepts only the exact newly reviewed sources;
it does not grant a whole shop host or authorize new recommendations.

Future, older-than-90-day and unresolved records keep their evidence and remove
merchant actions. The label is a model search, not an exact listing or verified
availability. Use only the confirmed `getecoback-21` tag; no ASIN is guessed.
The catalog and current counts are generated from reviewed data, not live stock.
Source-check dates and actual editorial dates stay separate; builds do not stamp
an unchanged catalog as newly edited.

## Discovery, measurement and release

Existing German home, household and tools entries link to the catalog, and the
shared German footer exposes it. Native category links and all product facts
work without JavaScript. Optional category/keyword filtering and category tools
run locally; search text and calculation inputs do not enter analytics. Successful
local tool work reuses the shared GA4 `tool_complete` vocabulary. The ECO first-party
collector now accepts that fixed name only on `/produkte.html`, with empty
metadata and DNT/GPC/automation exclusions; no inputs are accepted. Earlier absence
is unmeasured, not zero use. Merchant exits
reuse the existing `affiliate_click` listener and sanitized GA4 adapter; no new
property, query, job or synthetic click is introduced.

Static HTML, commerce-free Markdown and the existing read surfaces retain the
same exact source citations. Existing search/sitemap/language generation includes
the new canonical page. Collection/item-list schema describes only visible
entries, without fabricated offers or ratings. Final GA4 coverage, account
privacy, source-expiry gates, real mobile/desktop/no-JS browser checks and the
standard ECO release remain mandatory. IndexNow sends eligible actual changed
canonical URLs after deployment; receipt is separate from indexing or growth.

The existing owned-channel catalog/thermostat receipts and robot preparation
remain. No extra social post is submitted at the current cadence limit. Further
category batches need distinct useful products, readable sources and a relevant
tool; the manifest/builder provides the batch mechanism without a new scheduler,
paid dependency, automatic bulk publishing or new commercial account.

Five planning passes narrowed the owner's correction to product/category counts,
source/identity constraints, category tools, build/discovery integration and
real release acceptance. Two self-challenges rejected color/candidate inflation
and a generic tool copied across unrelated categories. Product implementation is
not proof that demand, traffic or merchant orders increased.
