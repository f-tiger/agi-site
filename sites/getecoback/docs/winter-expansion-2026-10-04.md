# German winter decision expansion

The owner commissioned a comprehensive expansion of the established winter
research directions. This is a bounded, authored release rather than the daily
one-page queue job. No new country storefronts, advertising spend, external
messages or paid offers are opened. Storage and balcony solar stay excluded.

## Evidence and scope

Primary consumer guidance was read on 4 October 2026: Verbraucherzentrale on
thermostats (article 7940), heating/ventilation (10426), hot-water hygiene (17755)
and decorative LED lighting (10902); co2online on window seals and showerheads.
Exact source URLs appear beside the applicable claims on each guide.
These sources establish practical decision constraints, not search volume or
Google ranking opportunity. A same-day public SERP feed returned unrelated
results and was unusable; no competitive-ranking verdict is asserted.
The stored German seasonality file fetched 27 September has a November peak
for radiator bleeding; it does not validate all seven new topics as popular.

Seven distinct intent pages cover: smart radiator-control payback, cold radiator
triage, window draughts, night setback, shower costs, home-office warmth, lighting
and timer costs. A task hub connects these to existing humidity, laundry, heated
blanket, curtain and meter-reading guidance. The existing infrared thermostat
page concerns electric-panel control; the new thermostat page concerns
water-radiator heads. The existing window seal pages concern portable AC ducts;
the new page concerns winter draughts. No duplicate product-review claims.

## Product and measurement

Free value: concrete checks, sources and three transparent calculators; no
account, input upload or device-performance promise. EUR/CHF selection changes
only display currency and requires all inputs in that currency. No FX feed.
Existing German affiliate category searches offer optional product discovery
following compatibility checks. There is no verified new merchant revenue.

Results explicitly distinguish examples from user-confirmed inputs. Only a
confirmed result emits the existing fixed `legacy:tool_complete` contract; export
emits `legacy:tool_export`. The shared optional Google channel owns consent,
withdrawal and affiliate clicks. Inputs and computed answers never enter events.
These new tool events are GA4-only; absence from the separate D1 collector is not
zero use. Browser mocks demonstrate frontend behavior, not backend receipt.

The public experiment uses 28 days after deployment. Fewer than 100 qualified
visits to the cohort means insufficient reach. At or above that operating
threshold, 10 confirmed tool completions or 5 affiliate clicks are continuation
signals, not statistical significance or revenue. Examine page/country cohorts;
confirm commissions separately using matching merchant records. Search Console
access remains needed for Google query and index diagnostics.
