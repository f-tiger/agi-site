# Put the reader's next decision before the next product card

The previous release aligned two model cards but left a path-level problem: the homepage foregrounded unrelated shelves, the air-quality hub sorted 37 guides by filename, diagnostic pages could advertise replacement devices before explaining the fault, and readers already ready to compare still had to answer a needs form. The cold-cellar result could reload its own page and clear the answers.

The change reuses existing pages and evidence. It does not add a catalog, laboratory ranking, country, paid tool or distribution job. Each reviewed route has one primary task and up to two reasoned onward links; unreviewed routes retain their existing behavior.

## What the German publishers demonstrate

The public pages establish available paths, not how many users complete them or their revenue contribution. Six linked journeys were checked across home&smart and CHIP on 6 November 2026. Commercial links were inspected without following them.

A central finder can propagate conflicting specifications. These observations support testing exact destinations and evidence boundaries, not copying the publishers' whole funnels.

## Product behavior

Homepage composition moves the existing household task block directly below the seasonal hero in autumn/winter, with maintenance next; summer/spring retain the BTU task first. Five discovery modules formerly after the footer return to the content. Secondary families, reference shelves, off-season reading and country links remain available through native disclosures. The existing blue visual system and authored links remain.

`moisture-journeys.json` owns exact reviewed page roles, next-step reasons and the air-category groups. Its visible directory order and labels also drive ItemList structured data. Eight reviewed measurement, diagnosis and repair guides suppress generic model, top-pick, sticky, popup, profile and area-based quick-pick promotions. The six chooser routes suppress sticky/profile shopping shortcuts that bypass their own market and need checks. Authored evidence and useful task links remain. This can reduce low-intent merchant clicks; the desired outcome is a useful task and appropriate onward decision, not the largest click count.

The shared next-step renderer is used by both the related-link builder and the later country-page generator, so measurement-page rebuilding cannot restore the previous three-link list. English headings and locale-specific destinations stay English. German default Germany and English default information-only market behavior remain.

The browser comparison has two speeds. Prepared readers open the existing comparison without changing their answers. Readers needing help keep all six outcomes. Static manufacturer evidence is open without JavaScript. A failed module leaves no submitting form. Cold-cellar links point to a verified section, and measurement/wait outcomes link to useful observation guidance. `buyer_next / compare / direct` never becomes `buyer_result` or tool completion; exact categorical metadata, opt-outs, QA exclusions and event deduplication remain enforced.

## Acquisition and measurement

The product priority is German humidity and indoor-laundry decisions plus adjacent usage/maintenance questions. Existing heating, cooling and international assets remain available. There is no new demand claim from competitor scale or a new country rollout. Seasonal promotion should promise one answer and link to that matching existing task; this release does not imply a new published post, video, merchant integration or paid campaign.

Preserve the existing November 5–18 observation window and mark this as an overlapping intervention. Record direct comparisons separately from guided results. Shared-entry exposure replaces form-only exposure; annotate this change in the `buyer_view` denominator. Search arrival, task use, evidence inspection, merchant clicks and attributable commission are separate measures. Technical checks, IndexNow acknowledgement and a smaller default page height do not establish ranking or revenue uplift.

Validation covers rebuild stability, retained article inventory, schema/visible-order agreement, genuine next-step anchors, mobile and desktop navigation, direct/guided comparison, no-JavaScript reading, preserved cold-room answers, locale boundaries and privacy. Production verification and deployment receipts are recorded separately.
