Where do I get this file?

In Shopify, open Settings → Languages → Export. Keep a backup. Upload that translation export, not the general product export. CSV is a plain-text table; you do not need to open it in a spreadsheet first.

Will this edit my store?

No. You review and import the CSV yourself. We never ask for your Shopify password or access token.

Does a passed check mean perfect translation?

No. Checks catch structural problems and missing details. A person still needs to review language, context and claims.

Is this a Shopify app?

This is an independent CSV workflow. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Shopify.
