Free checker

The CSV checker and fixed sample run in your browser. They do not upload your catalog, use advertising trackers or contact a model provider. The page checks whether paid processing is available; this request contains no CSV content.

If paid processing is opened

Starting a paid batch uploads the CSV and protected terms to our Cloudflare service. Only eligible source fields, their target languages and matching protected terms are sent to OpenRouter and the selected model provider. Do not upload customer records, personal information or confidential material you cannot share with these processors.

A daily salted hash of the network address limits batch starts; the raw address is not stored in the job database. Files, job records and translations expire after 30 days. A daily cleanup removes expired records. Your browser saves a recovery key locally; anyone holding that key can access the batch and request its refund. Keep the downloaded recovery file private. Payment details are entered on Stripe’s hosted checkout, not on this site. Stripe retains payment records under its own policies.

What a batch delivers

One fixed file, up to 500 content rows and 100,000 eligible characters, with no more than 500 characters per field. Only blank translations for product titles, SEO titles and SEO descriptions in supported languages are processed. Existing translations and unsupported content stay unchanged. There is no automatic store edit or promise of increased sales.

Automated checks can miss mistranslations, invented nonnumeric claims and language errors. You must review the output before importing it. A result passing checks is not a professional translation certification. The sample uses fictional catalog data and fixed translations; it does not demonstrate a live provider’s quality.

Payment and refunds

The proposed pilot price is €19 plus applicable tax per batch. The hosted checkout shows the final amount before payment. A batch is processed only after the server verifies payment. A seven-day full refund can be requested using its recovery key. This stops new work; a call already in progress may finish. Refund status is shown explicitly; a pending refund is not a confirmed bank credit.

Do not pay unless the live operator and support details are present and paid processing is explicitly available. No payment is currently collected by this site.

Independent product

LocaleBatch is an independent product translation workflow. It is not affiliated with Shopify, OpenRouter or Stripe.
