Rules you can audit

  1. Selected October 3, 2026 (Shanghai): use the October 2, 2026 regular-session close in New York. This previous-close baseline replaces the unstarted October 5 plan at the owner’s request; it is not a portfolio registered before entry. If any entry price is missing, the complete basket remains unavailable; its start is never shifted silently.
  2. USD 10,000 per portfolio. Twelve stocks receive exactly 1/12 each initially, fractional shares assumed; no rebalancing or later deposits. SPY, QQQ and TQQQ are separate 100% benchmark sleeves. SPY is a tradable S&P 500 ETF proxy, not the index itself.
  3. Vendor adjusted-close ratios proxy reinvested distributions and splits. Fund costs are already reflected in ETF prices. Trading costs, taxes and FX are excluded. The entry close is a valuation convention, not a promised execution price.
  4. Maximum drawdown is the largest close-to-close fall from a prior peak in this observation window, not an intraday or future worst-case loss. Excess return is a percentage-point difference from SPY, not risk-adjusted alpha. No annualization of this short record.
  5. Updates reuse the existing weekday post-close job. Every series must cover the same observed SPY sessions. On failed or partial data, the previous complete record is kept and flagged. Historical vendor corrections are logged. No silent removal of losers or substitution of tickers.
