**Are these live odds?**

No. Each run records a snapshot with an exact UTC timestamp and links to the market so you can check the current price yourself. A quoted price that pretends to be live is the single most common way odds coverage misleads people.

**Can I bet here?**

**How are the markets chosen?**

By a fixed keyword filter applied to whatever the venues' own search and series endpoints return that run, then a fixed venue whitelist and regular expression per series. The board can look thin in a quiet week, and it will say so rather than pad itself.
