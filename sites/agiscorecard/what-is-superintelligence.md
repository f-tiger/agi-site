## FAQ

**What is superintelligence?**

AI vastly beyond the best humans across essentially all domains — the end-state of Aschenbrenner's argument. Where AGI is roughly human-level, superintelligence dwarfs all human experts.

**When will superintelligence arrive?**

Aschenbrenner predicted the early 2030s, via an intelligence explosion following AGI. As of mid-2026 the chain hasn't started (AGI-2027 is open, the intelligence explosion is pending), so the timeline is speculative.

**How is superintelligence supposed to happen?**

Through an intelligence explosion: once AI can do AI research, it improves itself, compressing about a decade of progress into a year and vaulting from AGI to superintelligence.
