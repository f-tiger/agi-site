FAQ

Is Nvidia overvalued in 2026?

This page deliberately does not grade the price — no multiple, no target, no buy/sell call. What it grades is the bet structure: by the AGI Scorecard's published mapping, 90% of Nvidia's AI story rides on compute scaling and the capex wave, both currently graded supportive, and only 10% on the intelligence-explosion prediction, still pending. Whether that structure is worth the current price is a judgement this page leaves to the reader.

What would have to be true for Nvidia's AI bet to keep paying?

Two things, mostly: training compute keeps scaling on trend (graded On track as of the site's August 2026 review) and the AI capex wave keeps funding accelerators (graded Exceeded — spending overshot the prediction). Together those carry 90% of the mapped exposure. The remaining 10% rides on an intelligence explosion — automated AI research — still graded Pending. Nvidia is a buildout stock: it needs the spending to continue, not AGI itself to arrive.

No — the three most legible AI books on file disagree completely. And Aschenbrenner's Situational Awareness LP held roughly $1.57B notional in Nvidia puts in its Q1 2026 filing — before losing about 67% in July 2026 as the shorts moved against it, per CNBC and Bloomberg.
