Why embed one?

If your project, paper, or reading list touches AGI timelines, the badge answers the first question every reader has — where does the 2027 bet actually stand? — without them leaving your page. When a verdict flips (the pre-registered conditions are public), the badge updates everywhere at once. No script, no iframe, no tracking beyond a standard image request.

Rules

  • Free for any use; the underlying data is CC BY 4.0.
  • Badges are updated when verdicts change — don't cache-bust or re-host them, or you'll freeze a live verdict.
  • Want more than a badge? The countdown widget embeds the full live card, and /for-agents documents the raw data endpoints.

Frequently asked questions

How do I add the AGI 2027 badge to my README?

Paste one line of markdown: an image link to https://agiscorecard.com/badge/agi-2027.svg wrapped in a link to the verdict page. The /badge page has copy-paste snippets for both badges.

Do the badges update automatically?

Yes. They are static SVGs regenerated whenever a scorecard verdict changes, so every README embedding them shows the current verdict with no action on your part.

Are the badges free to use?

Yes — free for any use, commercial or not. The underlying dataset is CC BY 4.0; the badge itself just asks that you link it back to agiscorecard.com.
