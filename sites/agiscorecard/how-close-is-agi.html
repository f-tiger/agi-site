What's already here

The measurable state of play is strong. Frontier models perform at roughly college-graduate level on knowledge work (~83% GDPval), handle complex agentic coding (~80% SWE-Bench Pro), and run as agents in real workflows. On the capability curve, the trajectory Aschenbrenner described is On track.

The missing piece

The gap between "very capable assistant" and "AGI" is autonomy. The defining bar — a system that can autonomously conduct AI research end-to-end, the "drop-in remote worker" — has not been demonstrated. That single gap is why the headline AGI-2027 prediction is graded Open, not On track.

Frequently asked questions

How close are we to AGI?

Close on capability, not on autonomy. As of mid-2026 frontier AI reaches ~83% on GDPval and ~80% on SWE-Bench Pro, but no system autonomously conducts AI research end-to-end — the defining AGI bar.

What's still missing for AGI?

Autonomy. The gap between a very capable assistant and AGI is a system that can independently do AI research and own tasks end-to-end without human cleanup — undemonstrated as of mid-2026.

When will we reach AGI?

No consensus: forecasts range from 2026 (Musk) and 2027 (Aschenbrenner) to 2030 (Hassabis), 2033 (Metaculus), and 2047 (academic survey median). Expert medians have compressed from ~2060 to ~2033 in six years.
