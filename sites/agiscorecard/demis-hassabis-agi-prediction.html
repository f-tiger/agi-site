Demis Hassabis's AGI prediction: ~50% by 2030

Last updated: June 30, 2026 · Updated as verdicts change
By the AGI Scorecard team · methodology & independence
Transformative AI this decade — but not imminent. Google DeepMind CEO Demis Hassabis puts AGI at roughly 50% by 2030: more cautious than Aschenbrenner’s 2027, more aggressive than the academic-survey median (2047). As a frontier-builder he expects transformative AI within the decade while warning against over-hyping exact near-term dates.

Why his view carries weight

Hassabis leads one of the two or three labs actually building frontier systems, so his estimate reflects daily contact with what current models can and can't do. That vantage tends to price in the friction between benchmark capability and genuine autonomy — which is why he lands more cautious than single-author extrapolations like Aschenbrenner's 2027.

How it's holding up

As of mid-2026 the evidence is consistent with his window: capability is strong (~83% on GDPval, ~80% on SWE-Bench Pro) but autonomous AI research is undemonstrated. His 2030 horizon has years left to run, whereas the nearer calls — Aschenbrenner's 2027 and Musk's 2026 — face reality first.

Frequently asked questions

What is Demis Hassabis's AGI prediction?

The DeepMind CEO puts AGI at roughly 50% by 2030 — more cautious than Aschenbrenner (2027) and Musk (2026), more aggressive than the academic-survey median (2047).

Does Demis Hassabis think AGI is close?

Yes, but carefully. He expects transformative AI within the decade (~50% by 2030) while repeatedly warning against over-hyping near-term timelines.

Is Hassabis more reliable than Aschenbrenner on AGI?

He builds frontier systems daily, so his caution on the timing of full autonomy looks defensible as of mid-2026. Aschenbrenner has been closer on compute and capex trends. Both expect transformative AI this decade.
