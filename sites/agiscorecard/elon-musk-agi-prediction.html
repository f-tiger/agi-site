How to weight it

Musk anchors the earliest end of the public distribution. His claims come from a builder's conviction — but also a documented history of aggressive dates (full self-driving, humanoid robots) that arrived later than promised. That's the reason to treat his end-of-2026 call as the optimistic bound, not the base case.

How it's tracking

Musk's is the first serious call to face reality. As of mid-2026, nothing meets a serious AGI bar — agentic coding is strong (~80% SWE-Bench Pro) but no system autonomously conducts AI research. On current evidence, end-of-2026 looks unlikely; Aschenbrenner's 2027, which resolves by January 2028, has a little longer to play out. As of July 25, 2026, roughly five months remain on Musk's clock — the earliest expiry among all public AGI calls. We keep a live countdown on every dated prediction, his included.
