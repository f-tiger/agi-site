The method behind the guide

A famous voice is a starting point.

We help readers move from an interview to a question they can investigate. We keep the source, the speaker’s view and our interpretation separate.

What we record

  • Publisher, publication date, relevant timestamp and original link.
  • A short paraphrase, clearly separated from editorial scenarios and suggested actions.
  • Limits, commercial context and evidence that could change the assessment.
  • A source-review date that changes only after another review. No automated claim of freshness.

Questions before you start

Are the views verified?

We check the original interview and mark the relevant time. Verifying that a speaker expressed a view does not verify that the view is true.

Does Jarvis predict the future automatically?

It currently provides source-linked editorial guidance and action starting points. It does not generate AI probabilities, truth scores or personal assessments.

What is free, and what does membership buy?

Reading, original videos, local records and exports are free. Existing AGI membership adds cloud workspaces and version history, not automatic personal-report subscriptions.

Will saved views send me notifications?

No. You can keep local records, choose a review date and export a calendar entry. Cloud storage does not imply automatic alerts.
