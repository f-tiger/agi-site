Sources, rights and evidence

This library combines external learning references with original AGI project briefs. A source being listed is not a promise of earnings, a rights clearance for reuse, or an endorsement by its creator.

What we publish

We select references with an identifiable creator, a concrete task and a traceable source. Video references and written guides are labeled separately. Source review means reviewing the published page or description; it does not mean watching every frame or reproducing the workflow. We do not republish transcripts or download media.

Evidence levels

  • Source reviewed: link and published description checked.
  • Workflow tested: only used when a dated test and results exist. No current entry has this label.
  • Revenue verified: requires a defined period and auditable transaction evidence. No current entry has this label.

Playback and privacy

Official players load only after a user action. Loading shares network and device information with the provider, whose terms and privacy policy apply. We preserve player controls and source links. A blocked or removed video is not mirrored or bypassed. Local saved links are private to this browser and are not submitted for editorial publication.

Commercial separation

Free video access is separate from AGI paid cloud workspaces. Tool links in these briefs are ordinary links, not configured affiliate offers. We do not sell advertising inside players or claim a relationship with creators. Any future sponsorship must be labeled and must not change evidence labels.

Corrections and rights concerns

Use the AGI discussion area to flag an unavailable link or factual issue. Rights holders can open a repository issue linked below with the case URL and a non-sensitive explanation; do not post private identification documents. Reports are reviewed manually; no response-time promise is made. A disputed embed can be disabled while reviewed.
