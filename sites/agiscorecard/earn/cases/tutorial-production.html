The business brief

Potential buyer
A small software team repeatedly explaining the same setup to customers.
Defined delivery
One short task walkthrough, corrected captions and a version/date note.
Where you add value
Choose the task from a real support question. Show the exact clicks and the final result in the current product.
What could fail
A polished video about an unimportant task solves little. Product changes can make recordings obsolete.
Demand test
Give the draft to someone unfamiliar with the task. Count where they get stuck before offering a larger tutorial package.

A delivery you can check

  1. Choose one repeated support question and obtain demo-account permission.
  2. Write a short outline from the starting screen to the completed task.
  3. Record actual actions and edit pauses without hiding errors.
  4. Have a new user follow it; correct unclear steps and name a review date.

Acceptance gate

A new user completes the task with the tutorial. Do not label a vendor demo as our independently tested result.
