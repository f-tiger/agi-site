The business brief

Potential buyer

An operations team with repetitive questions and an approved answer library.

Defined delivery

A sandbox prototype, test-question register, failure log and handover instructions.

Where you add value

The useful work is defining what it must refuse, which sources it can use, and how a person takes over.

What could fail

A successful demo does not establish production reliability. Permissions, retries, monitoring and maintenance are separate work.

Demand test

Use 20 representative test questions, including missing answers and hostile instructions. Record accuracy and escalation; do not auto-send customer replies.



A delivery you can check

Collect approved example questions and answers without customer personal data.

Build a sandbox workflow with read-only access and a manual review step.

Test known, ambiguous, unsupported and adversarial questions.

Calculate execution and review cost, document failures and define ownership.

Acceptance gate

Publish the test set and its actual results to the client. Quote production support separately; no autonomous-revenue claim.
