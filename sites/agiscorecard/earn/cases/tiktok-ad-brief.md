The business brief

Potential buyer

A brand with an existing offer, approved product claims and usable source assets.

Defined delivery

Three distinct concept briefs with hooks, shot lists, rights notes and one agreed next test.

Where you add value

Give each concept a different reason to buy; changing colors or captions is not a meaningful new angle.

What could fail

Creative delivery does not guarantee ad approval or sales. Advertising spend, account access and campaign management are not included.

Demand test

Have the brand approve one concept and its claims before paying for production. Keep ad spend outside the production quote.



A delivery you can check

Record the actual offer, audience, landing page and approved claims.

Write three distinct hooks and map each to a proof shot.

Check music, likeness rights, AI disclosure and current platform policies.

Approve a production scope and define how the brand will assess the test.

Acceptance gate

The client approves the claims, rights and scope. Platform eligibility and campaign performance remain unverified.
