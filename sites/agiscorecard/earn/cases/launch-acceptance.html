Playwright: browser testing and automation
By microsoft/playwright · Original source is in English

The business brief

Potential buyer
A founder with a working staging site and a defined launch task.
Defined delivery
Up to five pages, three manually agreed journeys, screenshots, reproducible defects and one retest.
Where you add value
Verify the user can finish the intended task; Lighthouse and axe-core cover different subsets and cannot certify security or accessibility.
What could fail
Login, third-party widgets and payment sandboxes complicate tests. Our starter checks do not submit forms or create purchases.
Demand test
Show a sample report from an authorized demo site. Ask a buyer to name one blocked launch flow and fund a small fixed-scope review.

A delivery you can check

  1. Confirm authorization, staging origin, five pages and three journeys.
  2. Run the downloadable Playwright baseline and retain environment/time evidence.
  3. Manually review agreed tasks, keyboard navigation and error recovery; quote security work separately.
  4. Retest fixed defects and hand over unresolved items with owner and severity.

Acceptance gate

The three agreed journeys have recorded results and remaining defects are accepted. Automatic checks alone do not mean launch approval.
