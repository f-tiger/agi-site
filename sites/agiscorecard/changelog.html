What changed, and when

Last updated: August 16, 2026 · Updated as verdicts change
Everything that changed on the evidence layer, dated and real. Every entry corresponds to a shipped change; score history is machine-readable. If you would rather not check back: subscribers get one email when something that matters actually changes — a verdict, the score, a new tool — and silence otherwise.

Changes

2026-08-16The dataset now lives in git too
Every verdict and every Thesis Tracker reading is mirrored to a public GitHub repository, rebuilt nightly. It only changes when a grade changes — so its commit history is an independent, timestamped record of when we changed our mind.
2026-08-15Listed on the official MCP Registry
com.agiscorecard/agi-scorecard is now on registry.modelcontextprotocol.io — AI agents can discover and call the Thesis Tracker, verdicts, market ledger and site search natively, no auth.
2026-08-15First on-site subscriber
The inline form converted its first reader end-to-end (homepage footer, Aug 14). The address is stored first-party; it syncs to the newsletter the moment the beehiiv key is configured.
2026-08-14A dated status line on the most-read page
"When will AGI arrive?" now opens with the two numbers that actually move: 504 days until the nearest forecast is settled, and 45 days with the Thesis Tracker stuck at 62.5 — the disagreement is moving, the evidence is not.
2026-08-13English stock coverage completed
Every US-listed name SunWatch tracks now has an English page — 18 of 18 — plus a new /en/stocks index. The names that are still Chinese-only are listed and labelled as such rather than quietly left out.
2026-08-13The exposure tool now points at the one paid product
/ai-stock-exposure is the most-used tool on the site and had no path to SunWatch, the only subscription on this domain with a real price. It has one now — with the public hit-and-miss ledger linked, not a claim.
2026-08-13New tool: Your AGI Timeline
One slider places the year you expect AGI against nine real published forecasts — bull, base case or bear — with a shareable result. Free, embeddable.
2026-08-13Holdings-update promise made accurate
The invest pages no longer promise a same-day rewrite of every 13F. Holdings change only after a filing is read line by line — and subscribers hear the day the page changes.
2026-08-12Opinion hook on the Altman page
The second-most-cited page now asks where you land versus Altman's "few thousand days" — one tap onto the forecaster spectrum.
2026-08-11More English stock pages
Rocket Lab and SK hynix ADR added to the English invest pages (five now), with a fix so Chinese-named tickers render properly in English.

Server-side "human" pageviews turned out to include an unrecognised crawler fleet; real-reader counts now come from JS-executed pageviews, and a UA audit records who is actually knocking.
2026-08-09English stock pages on the invest console

2026-08-08MCP server live at /mcp

Frequently asked questions

How often does this page update?

Whenever something real ships: a verdict change, a score move, a new tool or dataset. Entries are dated and correspond to public commits — nothing is backfilled or invented.

What counts as a change worth logging?

Verdict flips, Thesis Tracker score moves, new tools and datasets, and structural site changes. Routine copy edits do not qualify.
