FAQ

Are these AI tools really free?
Yes. All seven run in the browser, need no sign-up, no account and no payment, and none of them ask for an email before showing you the result. Subscribing to the briefing is optional and comes after the answer, never before it.
Can I embed these tools on my own site?
Four of them, yes: the AGI Type Test, the AI Job Risk Check, The Future Bet and the AI Stock Exposure Check each have an embed mode (add ?embed=1 to the URL) that strips the site chrome and fits in an iframe. The embed carries one link back to AGI Scorecard. Copy-paste iframe code is on this page.
Where does the underlying data come from?
From /data.json, the machine-readable dataset of eight graded predictions from Leopold Aschenbrenner's Situational Awareness, each with a verdict, a pre-registered flip condition and primary sources. It is published under CC BY 4.0, so you can reuse it with attribution.
Do the tools tell me when AGI will arrive?
No, and no honest tool can. They do something narrower and more useful: they place your view against named public forecasters (Musk ~2026, Aschenbrenner 2027, Hassabis ~2030, Metaculus ~2033), and show which evidence would have to move for each of them to be right.
