The first half's biggest signals

Capability kept climbing. Knowledge-work performance (~83% on GDPval-style tasks) and agentic coding (~80% on SWE-Bench Pro) both sit near the top of the skilled-human range on scoped tasks — the clearest continuation of the trends the 2027 thesis extrapolates.

The inputs stayed on trend. Effective compute has roughly held its ~0.5 OOM/yr pace, and AI infrastructure spending continued to exceed the essay's projections — though revenue still lags the spend, which is the bear case to watch.

The moat thesis kept eroding. Open-weight models from DeepSeek and Qwen remained within roughly 3–6 months of the frontier — the prediction this scorecard grades Wrong, with knock-on effects for the US–China race and the case for a government Project.

The bar that matters hasn't moved. No system has autonomously conducted AI research end-to-end. Until that changes, AGI-by-2027 stays Open — strong trend, undemonstrated destination.

What the second half will decide

The 2027 deadline means the next 18 months are decisive. The things to watch: any credible demonstration of autonomous AI research (would move AGI-2027 toward fulfilled), a visible plateau in capability or compute (would move it toward Wrong), and any formal US government AGI initiative (would resolve the Project prediction). Frequently asked questions

How much AI progress has there been in 2026 so far?

Substantial but uneven: knowledge-work capability (~83% GDPval) and agentic coding (~80% SWE-Bench Pro) kept climbing, compute stayed on its ~0.5 OOM/yr trend, and capex exceeded projections — but autonomous AI research remains undemonstrated.

Which AGI predictions are on track in 2026?

Three of the eight tracked predictions: knowledge-work capability, compute scaling, and capex (exceeded). One is wrong (open source fading), two are open (AGI by 2027, the government Project), and two are pending.

Is AGI still possible by 2027?

The claim is still open. The input trends it depends on have held through mid-2026, but the defining milestone — AI autonomously doing AI research — hasn't been demonstrated. It resolves by January 1, 2028.

What should I watch in late 2026?

Three things: any demonstration of autonomous AI research, signs of a capability or compute plateau, and any formal US government AGI initiative. Each would move a major verdict.
