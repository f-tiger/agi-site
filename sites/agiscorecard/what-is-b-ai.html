FAQ

What is B.AI?

B.AI is an AI relay station launched by Justin Sun on April 12, 2026: one API key and one balance that resell access to Claude, GPT, Gemini and leading Chinese models (MiniMax, Kimi, GLM, DeepSeek), with wallet-based login, card or crypto payment, and agent-payment plumbing (x402, ERC-8004) built in.

Is B.AI an official partner of OpenAI, Anthropic or Google?

No partnership has been announced by any of the model providers as of August 2026. B.AI presents itself as a relay/aggregator; how its access arrangements sit with each provider's resale terms is publicly undocumented — treat that as an open question, not a verdict either way.

How many users does B.AI have?

Sun stated 1.7 million registered users (his own announcement; not independently audited). Heavy incentives — 50,000-point starter credits, 1:1 recharge matching, a stated daily $10,000 subsidy pool — mean registered users and paying demand are different numbers.

What does B.AI signal about the agent economy?

It is the largest named commitment yet to x402-style agent payments: real subsidy money now sits behind the rail. It does not yet prove demand — Chainalysis counted 100M+ x402 transactions by spring 2026 but roughly half looked like testing, and CoinDesk reported in March 2026 that organic demand wasn't there yet. Watch paying usage, not registrations.
