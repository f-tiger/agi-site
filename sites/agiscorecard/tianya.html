Before you start

Does this predict profit or tell me what to buy?

No. The workbench calculates scenarios from your inputs. Its thresholds are proposed operating rules, not model-validated success rates. Investment figures are paper-position calculations, not live prices or orders. Your review notes remain self-reported until independently checked.

What can I use for free?

Reading the research, local calculations, saving one copy in this browser, restoring it, and JSON import/export are free and do not require membership. Saving replaces the existing local copy. Import replaces the draft in the current tab and does not write to local storage until you choose Save. Export a backup first if you want to retain the current draft.

Is a free AGI account the same as cloud membership?

No. The shared Fleet registration entry gives you an identity for AGI community and Jarvis features. Registration does not grant paid cloud storage, make a payment, or subscribe you to marketing. The existing cloud membership uses a separate 64-character hexadecimal access key; back up that key.

What does cloud membership include?

The existing plan has a base price of 9 USDT for 30 days, plus the order-matching decimal and network fees. It includes 50 workspaces, 10 versions per workspace, 64 KiB per version and 5 MiB in total. There is no automatic renewal or extra AI allowance. Check availability and the exact order amount on the member page.

Does opening the member page upload my record?

No. The cloud button passes a preview of your record to the existing member page. Review it there and explicitly choose to save. Opening the page does not create an order or claim that a cloud save succeeded. Local calculations do not require an API key.

How fresh is the Tianya research?

The retained research snapshot is dated 2026-10-05. All 60 historical candidate statements remain unknown under the strict evidence gate. The separate ledger preserves 20 forecast endpoints; this page does not automatically score them or refresh them as new forecasts. The archive is currently in Chinese.
