Situational Awareness vs AI 2027: two 2027 forecasts compared

Last updated: June 30, 2026 · Updated as verdicts change
By the AGI Scorecard team · methodology & independence
Two roads to the same year. Aschenbrenner’s Situational Awareness (June 2024) and the later AI 2027 scenario both center on 2027 and on the same engine — AI automating AI research. One is a trend-extrapolation thesis; the other a detailed month-by-month scenario. Their shared core claim, AGI around 2027, is graded Open here.

What each one is

Situational Awareness is Leopold Aschenbrenner's 165-page essay (June 2024): an argument built on extrapolating orders-of-magnitude trends in compute, algorithmic efficiency, and "unhobbling," concluding AGI is "strikingly plausible" by 2027. AI 2027 is a later, widely-discussed scenario from researchers including former OpenAI staff — a concrete, narrative month-by-month depiction of how a fast takeoff could unfold around 2027.

Where they agree

  • The year. Both treat 2027 as the critical inflection point.
  • The engine. Both hinge on recursive AI R&D — AI systems automating AI research and accelerating their own progress.
  • The stakes. Both foreground national security and the destabilizing pace of an intelligence explosion.

How the shared claim is doing

The thing both stake out — AGI-level capability around 2027, driven by autonomous AI research — is exactly what this scorecard grades as Open. As of mid-2026, agentic coding is strong (~80% SWE-Bench Pro) but no system has autonomously conducted AI research end-to-end. The shared core resolves by January 1, 2028.
