The predictor's own bet: Aschenbrenner's fund

The author of "AGI by 2027" didn't just predict — he ran Situational Awareness LP to a reported 439% return for 2026 through June (>1000% since inception). His Q1 2026 13F showed a very specific thesis: long AI power & infrastructure (Bloom Energy $879M, CoreWeave, SanDisk) with ~$8.5B notional in puts against chip names — Nvidia (~$1.57B), SMH (~$2B), Broadcom, AMD, Micron. Then July 2026: per CNBC and Bloomberg, the fund lost ~67% in a month as longs and shorts moved against it together, and transferred most of its public portfolio to Citadel — assets down from a ~$45B peak to ~$10B, keeping private stakes like Anthropic. He may still be right about the industry; the leverage didn't survive long enough to find out. Frequently asked questions

How are the biggest investors positioned on AI in 2026?

Per Q2 2026 SEC 13F filings (holdings as of June 30, filed August 14): Warren Buffett's Berkshire added Alphabet again — now 12.6% of the book across both share classes, its clearest AI bet — while staying out of Amazon; Cathie Wood is aggressively long AI infrastructure and next-gen compute; David Tepper leans bullish with Amazon at 15.4% his largest position; Bill Ackman remains bullish per his latest Q1 filing; Druckenmiller, Duan Yongping and Philippe Laffont are cautious; Michael Burry is the clearest bear.

What is Aschenbrenner betting on AI?

Leopold Aschenbrenner — who predicted AGI by 2027 — runs Situational Awareness LP. Its Q1 2026 13F showed the fund long AI power and infrastructure (Bloom Energy, CoreWeave) with roughly $8.5B notional in put options against chip names including Nvidia. In late July 2026, per CNBC and Bloomberg reporting, the fund lost about 67% in a month and transferred most of its public equity portfolio to Citadel; assets fell from a ~$45B peak to about $10B, retaining private stakes such as Anthropic.
