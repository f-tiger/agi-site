Count paid orders. Subtract refunds from revenue. Exclude cancelled orders. Compare net revenue with the previous week. Name the leading channel and say whether these rows prove ads caused growth.

All rows describe a fictional online store in USD. Use your own spreadsheet or permitted AI; never paste private store or customer data into the coach.

    Know what is being measured

    Who is this coach for?

    Adults starting in ecommerce operations who need to check a weekly store report. This first track trains order boundaries, refunds, channel totals and defensible growth explanations. Start with a baseline, fix one weak point, attempt a fresh case and recheck after three days.

    How do the levels work?

    Level 0: no completed case. Level 1: all five checks pass on any case. Level 2: first-attempt success on a new transfer case without mentor hints. Level 3: pass another case at least three days after an earlier independent pass, using the same allowed tools. Self-reported work application is shown separately.

    Is a higher score proof that the AI improved my work?

    No. These are controlled arithmetic and interpretation checks on fictional cases, not a validated job-skills assessment. Case difficulty, tools and practice differ. Time is an active-tab estimate for this visit, not a productivity benchmark. The AI explanation can be wrong; check the underlying rows.

    Where do my records go?

    By default, practice records stay in this browser. Clearing site storage removes them; export a backup first. An AI request sends your question, fictional case and answers to this site and Cloudflare Workers AI; this app does not save the question or reply to its database. Cloud saving is a separate action on the member page. Optional usage counts store event categories with a pseudonymous browser identifier for up to 90 days, not your report answers. Do not use this demo for sensitive data.

    Can I use this with learners or colleagues?

    Yes. Share this page or embed it with ?embed=1. Ask learners to keep their tool choice consistent and attempt a fresh case before comparing results. This is designed for adults.
