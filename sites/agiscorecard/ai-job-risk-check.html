How the tiers work

🟢 Sheltered (for now) — your work sits behind the strongest shields: physical presence, human trust, and end-to-end accountability.
🟡 Task-exposed — parts of your job overlap what AI already does well, but the core still needs a human in the loop.
🟠 Benchmark-adjacent — much of your work looks like what's measured on GDPval (~83%) and SWE-Bench Pro (~80%). The autonomy gap is your main shield.
🔴 In the crosshairs — screen-based, structured, reviewable work with little trust or judgment moat. Exposure is already here; replacement waits on the autonomy gap.

FAQ

How does the AI job exposure check work?
Six questions score the parts of work that 2026 evidence says matter: how screen-based and structured your tasks are (AI already reaches ~83% on GDPval knowledge-work tasks and ~80% on SWE-Bench Pro agentic coding), versus the shields — physical presence, human trust, and unsupervised end-to-end judgment, which no system has reliably crossed. You get an exposure tier, not an invented percentage.
What is the autonomy gap?
The gap between scoring well on benchmarks and reliably owning a whole job end-to-end without supervision. As of mid-2026 no AI system has crossed it — it is the single line that decides when task exposure becomes actual job replacement, and it is what the AGI Scorecard tracks.
Is this based on real data?
The rubric is transparent and grounded in documented evidence tracked on this site: ~83% on GDPval-style knowledge work, ~80% on SWE-Bench Pro, and the undemonstrated autonomy milestone (AGI-2027 Thesis Tracker at 62.5/100). It returns a tier based on your own answers — it does not invent per-job statistics.
