Train & inspect locally Exact thresholds, assumptions & limitations RSI proxy: capability ≥ 0.72, autonomy ≥ 0.55 and feedback multiplier ≥ 1.55 for four quarters. SI proxy: after RSI, capability ≥ 0.92 and autonomy ≥ 0.80 for four quarters. These definitions are modeling choices, not agreed scientific standards. The model starts at 2026.75 with capability 0.32 and autonomy 0.13. Coefficients have not been fitted to a historical outcome set. There are no verified RSI/SI outcome labels for calibration. It does not estimate unemployment, geopolitical incidents, company returns or existential risk. Governance only models deployment speed here, not the benefits of safety.
