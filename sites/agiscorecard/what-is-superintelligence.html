What is superintelligence? And when might it arrive?

Last updated: June 30, 2026 · Updated as verdicts change
By the AGI Scorecard team · methodology & independence
AI far beyond the best humans — the end-state of the AGI argument. Superintelligence is AI vastly beyond the best humans across essentially all domains. In Situational Awareness it follows AGI via an intelligence explosion (AI improving AI), arriving in the early 2030s. As of mid-2026 that chain hasn’t started, so the timeline is speculative.

The definition

Where AGI is roughly human-level (able to do the work of, say, an AI researcher), superintelligence is far beyond the best humans across nearly every domain. It's the difference between "matches an expert" and "dwarfs all experts combined."

How it's supposed to arrive

Aschenbrenner's mechanism is the intelligence explosion: once AI reaches the level of a competent AI researcher (the AGI-2027 milestone), you point it at improving AI itself. Hundreds of thousands of automated researchers compress roughly a decade of progress into about a year — vaulting from AGI to superintelligence.
