— Leopold Aschenbrenner, former OpenAI Superalignment researcher, in Situational Awareness (June 2024)

Evidence review, November 25, 2026

The verdict stays Open. The sources reviewed here document narrower demonstrations, improvements on research tasks and findings under lab safety policies. None establishes the general research capability required for Right in the published criteria.

  • November 20, independent capability evaluation. METR found that the estimated task-completion time horizon on its combined TH1.1 and TH2 suites roughly doubled every 3.6 months. In a separate exercise within the same report, agents produced evaluation tasks that METR judged insufficiently useful to include. These results describe performance on the tasks studied. METR, Q4 capability update
  • November 23, OpenAI system card. OpenAI assessed GPT-6.2 in ChatGPT at High for AI Self-Improvement, which describes assistance comparable to a strong mid-career research engineer. The framework defines a separate Critical tier. System card · Preparedness Framework v2
  • November 10, Anthropic filing, and November 24, independent synthesis. Anthropic determined that it met its AI research and development policy threshold based on what it expects from future models and its overall rate of progress. FRI compared the threshold definitions and reviewed evidence on research assistance and autonomy. Anthropic, Form S-1/A No. 2 · Forecasting Research Institute
  • November 24, METR on pretraining loss. Faster improvement on one held-out code corpus has several possible explanations. METR lists broad capability acceleration over the next six months as one interpretation and says there is no consensus on how the results change capability forecasts. METR analysis
  • A narrow demonstration relevant to Partial. Sakana AI reported that one of three generated manuscripts passed review at an ICLR workshop. The team withdrew the paper under the study protocol. In an October CRUX follow-up, expert reviewers rejected both manuscripts from two open-ended AI research runs. These studies cover different tasks and offer evidence to consider at resolution. Sakana AI study in Nature · CRUX follow-up

This review adds sources and keeps the existing status. The final evaluation will apply the published criteria for Right, Partial and Wrong. The dated Thesis Tracker score and original citation asset remain historical records.

What would settle it

By January 1, 2028, the scorecard uses its pre-registered criteria for Right, Partial and Wrong. Narrow autonomous research demonstrations can count towards Partial. The rule for Right calls for credible public evidence that frontier models can autonomously perform the work of an AI researcher or engineer.

Frequently asked questions

Will AGI arrive by 2027?

Open as of November 25, 2026. The reviewed sources document progress on specific research tasks and assistance to researchers. They do not establish that a model can take on the work of an AI researcher or engineer under the published criteria. The deadline is January 1, 2028.

What evidence would settle the prediction?

Read the pre-registered criteria for Right, Partial and Wrong. The test is autonomous AI research under the claim, with a resolution deadline of January 1, 2028.

How should I compare other AGI timelines?

Treat the comparison as historical context. Forecasters use different capability definitions, and a date is not necessarily a median probability estimate.
