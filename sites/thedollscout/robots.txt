# DollScout — thedollscout.com
#
# AI crawlers are welcome here, deliberately. Most sites in adjacent categories
# block them; we do the opposite. Paid search and paid social are closed to this
# category, and "is this shop legitimate?" is a question people increasingly ask
# an assistant rather than a search box. Being the source those answers cite is
# a channel our competitors cannot buy either — and getting cited requires being
# readable. See /llms.txt for a structured index.
#
# /legal/ is deliberately NOT disallowed here, and that is the opposite of what
# this file said until 2026-08-16. Those three pages carry
# <meta name="robots" content="noindex, follow"> and are excluded from
# sitemap.xml, which is the correct way to keep boilerplate out of the index.
#
# Disallowing them as well was actively harmful. A crawler forbidden from
# FETCHING a page never sees the noindex on it — so it kept the URLs, which it
# found from the site-wide footer link on all 43 pages, and indexed them
# URL-only. Bing Webmaster Tools showed exactly three indexed URLs for this
# domain, against three files in /legal/, and listed "legal" as the only
# subfolder it knew. The only pages Bing had indexed were the three we had told
# it not to.
#
# The methodology and the money — the things that actually matter for trust —
# are published openly at /trust.
