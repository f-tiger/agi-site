# Outreach kit — the owner's copy-paste pack

Internal (`content/` is never published). Built 2026-07-29 from live search
research; every target below was verified to exist and be active this year.

**Why this document exists.** The site has zero human traffic and, in this
category, traffic has exactly four legal doors: organic search (working —
crawled and ranking for the brand already, but slow), AI-assistant citations
(seeded via llms.txt/robots), **being cited by people who already have an
audience**, and email (blocked on a provider). This kit is door three. It is
the highest-leverage thing available and it is also the one thing that cannot
be automated: **every message below must be sent by you, as a human, from your
own account.** Automated outreach in this niche reads as spam, gets screenshot,
and would burn the only asset we have.

**The rules (non-negotiable, same as the site's):**
- No affiliate links in anything you offer them. The for-creators page is built
  that way on purpose — it is *why* they can say yes.
- Offer data, never ask for a link. If the data is good they link it themselves;
  asking converts a gift into a transaction.
- No incentives, no "collab", no commission-sharing offers. One screenshot of
  that offer ends the trust positioning permanently.
- If they ignore you, do not follow up more than once. Silence is an answer.

## What you are offering (the asset list)

Three things nobody else in the niche publishes, each answering a question
their audience asks weekly:

1. **The weight dataset** — 33 real listings; headline: height barely predicts
   weight, build does; a slim 151cm doll was lighter than every 140cm doll in
   the sample. → `/guides/height-weight.html`
2. **The import-cost correction** — the US $800 de minimis is gone (Aug 2025,
   indefinite June 2026) and almost every guide still says otherwise; 11
   country pages. → `/guides/import-costs-2026.html`
3. **The payment recourse checker** — statute-sourced s.75/chargeback logic,
   deposit-covers-the-whole-price rule, no affiliate links on the page at all.
   → `/payment-protection.html`

The pitch is never "look at my site". It is "here is the number you needed for
your next video, sourced, free, no strings".
