Which blind-box probability model fits?Use independent-draw math only when each box has the same chance and previous outcomes give no information about the next one.Check a toy retailer before payingBegin with the official regional store or directory, then verify the exact seller and item. Product photos and a familiar logo do not establish authorization.Measure a display that actually fitsUse the empty inside space and the full outline of the object in its intended pose. Outside box dimensions and nominal toy height are not enough.Start a collection you can keep enjoyingChoose a small theme, a spending limit and a place to display it. A clear wish list is more useful than treating every new release as a missing piece.
