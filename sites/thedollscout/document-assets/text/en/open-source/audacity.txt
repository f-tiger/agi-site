Record and edit speech before transcription.

Audacity is a desktop audio editor for recording, trimming and mixing. It is useful for preparing a short speech clip and listening closely to mistakes.

The linked tutorial was published in 2023; current controls can differ. Review current project documentation. Audacity’s distribution is GPLv3; individual files and components may have other licenses.

Select the intended microphone and make a short recording.

Listen for clipping and room noise; adjust the source first.

Trim an English sample to 60 seconds or less and export WAV/MP3.

Try the TDS transcript and compare it with the recording.
