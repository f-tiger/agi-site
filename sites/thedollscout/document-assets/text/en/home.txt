Free tools. Files stay on your device.

No account or file upload is needed. Keep your original files unchanged and download the results you need. Each tool explains its limits before you start.

Are all six tools free?

Yes. PDF checks, batch review, text extraction, comparison, file verification and delivery records are free to use. No account is required.

Do you upload or store my files?

File processing runs in your browser. Files and document text are not uploaded. If you choose to share a verification link, it contains a SHA-256 fingerprint and byte count; full exports can contain filenames and your notes.

Which tool checks whether a file has changed?

File verification compares SHA-256 and byte count with a sender-provided reference. Text comparison checks extracted wording instead. Neither tool proves identity, delivery, acceptance or malware safety.
