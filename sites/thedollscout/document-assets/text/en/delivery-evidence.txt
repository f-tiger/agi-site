# Build a client delivery record

https://thedollscout.com/delivery-evidence
Published by TDS Document Scout
Updated: 2026-09-25

Keep the exact files, agreed scope and client response together. Create a portable handoff record in your browser.

Up to 10 files, 20 MB each, 100 MB total. Files and notes stay in this tab; nothing is uploaded or saved automatically.

Save the agreed scope and its original email or contract.

Keep the exact delivered files and the original delivery message.

Record the client’s actual response, including changes requested.

Keep missing or disputed facts visible; never fill them in from a guess.

A SHA-256 fingerprint identifies file bytes. Notes and creation time come from you and your device. TDS does not verify authorship, recipient identity, receipt, acceptance or payment authorization. No Bitcoin timestamp or signature is created.

For payment disputes, relevant evidence depends on the dispute reason. Delivery evidence alone does not establish that a cardholder authorized a purchase. Check your processor’s current requirements.
