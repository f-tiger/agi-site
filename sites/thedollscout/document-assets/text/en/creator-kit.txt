Start with the computer and equipment you already own. Upgrade only when a recording or editing test reveals a specific problem.

As an Amazon Associate, TDS earns from qualifying purchases. The links below open Amazon search results, not a tested product ranking. Prices, compatibility and delivery must be checked on the retailer page. EN and ZH links use Amazon US; DE links use Amazon Germany.

Clearer speech

Move closer to your existing microphone, reduce room noise and record a short sample in Audacity.

Check the connector, operating-system support, return policy and whether direct headphone monitoring is included. A microphone cannot guarantee accurate transcription.

More consistent portraits

Face a window and use a plain background. Try the portrait cutout with your existing photo first.

Check power supply, brightness control, stand inclusion and available space. Lighting is optional; the cutout tool does not require special equipment.

Room for video projects

Remove disposable exports and check free disk space before adding another drive.

Check your computer’s ports, usable capacity and cable inclusion. Keep a separate backup; an external drive alone is not a backup plan.
