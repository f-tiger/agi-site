# Image compressor & converter

Published by TDS Document Scout
Updated: 2026-09-27

Resize images and export JPEG, PNG or WebP. Compare actual dimensions and file sizes before downloading each result.

Your files, pasted data and meeting details are processed in this browser. No account or upload is needed. Downloaded results can contain your input; review them before sharing.

Up to 8 still JPEG, PNG or WebP images; 20 MiB each, 50 MiB total, 20 megapixels each. No GIF, HEIC, SVG or animation. Output is limited to 4096 × 4096 pixels and never enlarged.

The browser decodes and redraws each image, preserving proportions. JPEG uses a white background for transparency. PNG ignores the quality setting. Re-encoding can change colours or increase file size; this is not a print-quality or metadata-erasure guarantee.

A 1500 × 1000 image fitted within 1000 × 1000 becomes 1000 × 667. The original stays unchanged. The interactive sample measures its actual exported size.

1500 × 1000 → 1000 × 667

Does compression always make an image smaller?

No. Output size depends on the source, chosen format, dimensions and browser encoder. The result shows both byte counts, including an increase when the output is larger.

Are transparent images supported?

PNG and WebP can preserve transparency. JPEG cannot; transparent pixels are placed on a white background. Animated files are rejected.
