What facts can you quote from this site?

Everything here is checkable. If you find one of these wrong, tell us and we will correct it publicly.

  • Height barely predicts weight — build does. Across 33 current listings we recorded, 150–151 cm dolls ran from 43 lb (slim B-cup) to 94 lb (heavy M-cup), and a slim 151 cm doll was lighter than every 140 cm doll in the sample. Full chart and method →
  • "Torso" hides a five-fold range. Small torsos from 13 lb; large 140 cm-class torsos 62–65 lb — as heavy as some complete dolls.
  • The US $800 duty-free allowance is gone. Suspended for all countries on 29 August 2025 (EO 14324), made indefinite for non-postal shipments in June 2026. Germany dropped its €150 threshold on 1 July 2026. Sources →
  • Nobody can honestly quote a US duty rate for a full-size doll. The tariff classification is unsettled. The useful question is DDP or DDU.
  • A 50%+ discount is a counterfeit signal, not a bargain. Factories set minimum pricing; legitimate vendors sit roughly 10–15% below list.
  • Routine care costs 15–30 minutes after every use, before any monthly work. That number, not the word "maintenance", is what decides TPE vs silicone.
  • The TPE-vs-silicone hygiene difference has a measured number. In the one published study we could find (2014, n=12, vibrators not dolls), HPV DNA was still detectable on a thermoplastic-elastomer surface 24 hours after cleaning with a commercial cleaner in 40% of positive samples — and on silicone in none. Limits and source on the page; it matters for shared and second-hand use, not solo. Study, limits and what it changes →
  • A credit-card deposit protects the whole price, not the deposit. UK s.75 runs on the item's cash price (over £100, up to £30,000), so a £300 deposit on a £1,800 made-to-order doll is covered for the doll — read directly from the statute and the Financial Ombudsman, not from forum advice.
