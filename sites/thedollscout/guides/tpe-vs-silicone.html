TPE vs Silicone: the honest comparison

The industry's most-asked question, answered without a sales agenda.

The one-question decision rule: "Will I actually spend 15–30 minutes cleaning and drying after each use — in year three?" If yes — TPE gives you the softest feel for hundreds less. If you're honest with yourself and the answer is no — buy silicone or don't buy at all. An unmaintained TPE doll degrades, stains and can grow mold.

What do shops not tell you about each material?

  • Cost per year beats sticker price. A $2,400 silicone doll kept 6 years costs less per year than a $1,200 TPE doll that degrades in 3. Run your numbers in the True Cost Calculator.
  • Hybrids exist and are a smart middle path: silicone head (durable makeup, best facial realism) on a TPE body (soft, cheaper). Many factories offer this configuration.
  • "Silicone" under ~$1,000 full-size is a red flag. Real platinum silicone is expensive raw material. Impossible prices mean TPE mislabeled as silicone — or a counterfeit shop.
  • Weight is similar in both materials — material choice won't save you from the weight problem. A full-size doll is 55–100+ lb either way; if that's a concern, consider a torso or compact build first.

Which one should you actually choose?

  • First doll, budget under $1,500: compact TPE from a verified factory brand — or a premium torso to learn your preferences cheaply.
  • Budget $1,800+, or you hate maintenance: full silicone.
  • Best facial realism on a budget: hybrid (silicone head + TPE body).
