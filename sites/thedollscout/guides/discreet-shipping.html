Discreet shipping, customs & privacy — what actually happens

The anxieties nobody asks out loud, answered specifically.

What does the box look like?

  • Reputable vendors ship in a plain brown cardboard crate with no product names, no brand logos, no images. The shipping label shows a neutral company name (often a generic trading or logistics name).
  • The crate is big and heavy — a full-size doll arrives in a box roughly 5 ft long, 60–120 lb. Neighbors will see a large box, nothing more. Plan how you'll get it inside alone; some owners schedule delivery for a workday morning or use a pickup point.
  • Torsos and compact items ship in ordinary parcel boxes — far easier to receive discreetly.

What shows up on your card statement?

  • Vetted vendors use neutral billing descriptors — a generic company name, not a product description. Ask support before ordering: "What descriptor appears on my statement?" A legitimate shop answers directly.
  • Extra insulation: pay via PayPal (statement shows PayPal + a neutral merchant name) — which also gives you the 180-day dispute window.

How long does delivery actually take?

  1. Production: 1–4 weeks. Made-to-order is normal. Silence during production is normal too.
  2. Factory photos → your approval. The step you never skip. Nothing ships until you approve.
  3. Freight: 1–3 weeks. Sea/air freight, then local courier. Tracking may stall for days at customs — that's normal.
  4. Delivery. Some couriers require a signature for high-value crates. If discretion matters, arrange a delivery day you're home alone or use a depot pickup.

What do you do if the crate arrives damaged?

  • Film the unboxing before you open it. Continuous video from sealed crate to unwrapped doll is the evidence that wins disputes.
  • Note visible crate damage with the courier at delivery if possible.
  • Document everything in photos, contact the vendor in writing immediately, and know your fallback: PayPal dispute (180 days) or card chargeback. Minor issues (a loose finger wire, small tears) are commonly self-repaired — see the care guide.

Vendors whose shipping practice we've checked

Discretion is a vendor policy, not an industry standard — plain outer crate, neutral sender name, no product description on the label. It is worth asking before you order, and worth walking away over.
