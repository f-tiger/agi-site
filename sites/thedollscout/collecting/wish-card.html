How someone can respond
  1. Choose the exact item, general region, budget and condition. Only list an offer you are ready to trade.
  2. Review the card and copy text or download a PNG. Publish it yourself where wanted posts are permitted.
  3. Keep replies in that original community thread. Record a response or closure here for your own reference.
Why this tool exists
Collectors have asked for an overall wishlist and a list to use while shopping or trading. These discussions show a task, not a promise that someone will fulfill your request.
