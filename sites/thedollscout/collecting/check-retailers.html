Check a toy retailer before paying

Begin with the official regional store or directory, then verify the exact seller and item. Product photos and a familiar logo do not establish authorization.

Published by The Doll Scout · Sources checked: 2026-10-02

Trace the store from the brand

Open the brand website yourself and follow its store links. For Sonny Angel, the official directory separates regions and store types. For Jellycat, begin at its official site and Store Locator. Check the destination domain before supplying personal details.

Name the seller of record

On a marketplace, the platform and seller can differ. Save the shop name, product reference, condition and whether the item is sealed or opened. Compare the final seller shown at checkout with the listing.

Ask a question that can be checked

For a confirmed item, request the exact character, series, included card or accessories and condition photos. For a sealed box, ask which series and package format it is. A checklist helps surface inconsistencies but cannot certify authenticity.

Compare the delivered total

Include postage and any applicable fees in the quote. Read cancellation and return terms on that seller’s current page; do not assume another store’s terms apply. If material details remain unclear, pause the purchase instead of filling gaps with guesses.
