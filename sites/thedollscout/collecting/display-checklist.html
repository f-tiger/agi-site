Measure a display that actually fits

Use the empty inside space and the full outline of the object in its intended pose. Outside box dimensions and nominal toy height are not enough.

Published by The Doll Scout · Sources checked: 2026-10-02

Measure the usable opening

Record inside width, depth and height in one unit. Exclude rails, door hinges and other obstructions. Check the opening you must move the object through as well as the space where it will sit.

Include the whole silhouette

Measure base, ears, outstretched parts and accessories. For plush, measure the pose you intend to keep without assuming it can be compressed. One dimension from a product title cannot describe its full footprint.

Read the layout honestly

The calculator compares uniform rows in one orientation with uniform rows rotated 90 degrees. It does not mix rotations or prove the mathematically best packing. Height must fit, and spacing applies between items rather than around the outer border.

Test before committing

Use paper footprints for a quick physical check. Leave practical handling room beyond a tight mathematical fit. Check the display maker’s load and installation instructions separately; the calculation does not assess weight, anchoring or stability.
