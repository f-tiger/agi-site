Your preparation card

Your reviewed activity template or participant card will appear here.

Before sharing

No account is needed. This tool does not save drafts or change your collection. The share link contains the activity and chosen figure names as readable data, not an encrypted or revocable secret. Anyone with it can read, change or forward it. Do not include private contact details.

A preparation card is not a registration, match, reply, ownership proof or completed trade. Arrange any response in the place where the link was shared, and confirm the organizer’s current rules.

Use one link with your group

  1. An organizer prepares an activity template with its own rules. No participant’s collection is included.
  2. Each participant opens that link and chooses their own wanted and offered names.
  3. Review the result, then share it where the activity allows. Recipients can create a separate card without copying your choices.

For organizers

You may link to this free tool from your own activity information and distribute your reviewed template. Keep the rules specific to your event. This does not create a TDS or brand partnership.
