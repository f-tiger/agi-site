Who makes Labubu?

Labubu was created by Hong Kong-born artist Kasing Lung for his illustrated series The Monsters (2015), and is produced under exclusive license by Pop Mart since 2019 (Wikipedia). It became popular enough that the counterfeits earned their own name — Lafufu (Gulf News) — which is exactly why the rarity chase and the authenticity checks belong on the same site.
