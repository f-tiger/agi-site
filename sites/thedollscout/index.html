Why do first-time buyers get burned?

We built this from documented complaint patterns — the recurring ones, not a headcount we never took. Four traps come up again and again.

🪤

Counterfeit "brand" dolls

Scam sites steal official factory photos, then ship a cheap knock-off — wrong face, wrong skin tone, broken skeleton. By then your money is gone.

⭐

Fake & extorted reviews

Paid 5-star reviews, and vendors who only fix problems in exchange for good ratings. Public review scores in this niche can't be trusted at face value.

💸

Hidden real costs

Import duties, care supplies, storage, repairs. A "$1,500 doll" is rarely a $1,500 decision — and nobody shows you the math up front.

🏋️

The weight surprise

The #1 owner regret isn't looks — it's buying a doll too heavy to move, pose, or store. Almost no shop surfaces weight as a primary filter. We do.

📦

Shipping anxiety

Weeks of waiting, customs worries, damaged crates, and fear the box gives it away. We verify discreet-packaging claims before recommending anyone.

🤷

Choice paralysis

Near-identical listings, TPE vs silicone jargon, brand soup. Our 60-second finder cuts it down to the 2–3 options that actually fit you.

Is buying from this site anonymous?

We're a guide, not a shop — you never buy from us. Vendors we recommend are vetted for unmarked packaging and neutral billing descriptors, and our discreet-shipping guide shows exactly what the box and your card statement will look like.

How do you make money?

If you buy through our links, the vendor pays us a referral fee at no extra cost to you. That's the entire business model, and it's disclosed on every page. We recommend against vendors that fail vetting even when they offer higher commissions.

TPE or silicone?

Short version: TPE if budget matters most and you'll keep up with maintenance; silicone if you want durability and easy cleaning. Long version with photos and a comparison table is in our guide.

What's the single biggest first-buyer mistake?

Underestimating weight. A full-size doll can weigh 60–100 lb of dead weight. Take the finder honestly — it asks about weight tolerance before anything else.
