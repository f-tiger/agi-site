LABUBU / STYLE ODDS

Which one are you hoping for?

Calculate the chance of your chosen style before opening another box.

Editable example, not official odds. Start with your package.

Example: 12 independent boxes at secret odds of 1:72 give a 15.45% chance of at least one secret.

Model and limits

The regular-style model assumes the secret replaces a regular style and every regular style has the same chance: p = (1 − 1/N) ÷ K. The example uses K = 6 and N = 72. The official source confirms style names; it does not establish these model assumptions.

If your package states a probability for the exact style, choose “Enter a known per-style probability”.

Independent draws only. A sealed no-repeat case or a POP NOW box with hints needs different information. Missing a style earlier does not make the next box luckier. Set a budget before buying.

How the estimate works

This is a uniform rectangular grid, not a maximum mixed-orientation packing plan. No stacking, weight or stability check. Gaps apply between pieces, not at the shelf edges. Measure usable inner space and allow any extra top or edge clearance yourself.

Know the series before the secret.

  1. Identify the release

    Match the full series name, figure or plush format, dimensions and package contents against the official listing. A familiar character name is not enough.

  2. Read the actual odds

    Use the probability printed for that series. Our Labubu odds reference links its sources; it is not a live stock or resale-price feed.

  3. Choose how to buy

    Compare a sealed single box, a complete set and a confirmed character. Independent-box probability is not a guarantee of a case assortment.
