Know the series before the secret.

  1. Identify the release

    Match the full series name, figure or plush format, dimensions and package contents against the official listing. A familiar character name is not enough.

  2. Read the actual odds

    Use the probability printed for that series. Our Labubu odds reference links its sources; it is not a live stock or resale-price feed.

  3. Choose how to buy

    Compare a sealed single box, a complete set and a confirmed character. Independent-box probability is not a guarantee of a case assortment.
