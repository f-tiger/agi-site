Model and limits

Regular-style mode assumes one secret replaces a regular style and all K regular styles have equal probability: p = (1 − 1/N) ÷ K. Enter K and the printed secret denominator N. This assumption is not a verified brand rate. For several secret variants, use the exact per-style percentage instead of a combined secret rate. Use the exact per-style percentage when available.

If your package states a probability for the exact style, choose “Enter a known per-style probability”.

Independent draws only. A sealed no-repeat case or a POP NOW box with hints needs different information. Missing a style earlier does not make the next box luckier. Set a budget before buying.

Start with the series. Check the seller.

  1. Identify the series

    Compare the series name, package and whether the sale is sealed or opened. Do not assume every listing is the same kind of blind box.

  2. Check the regional directory

    The official store page separates online stores, distributors and retailers by region. Follow its current links rather than a sponsored search result alone.

  3. Keep a duplicates plan

    Record owned and wanted pieces before another purchase. Budget for a sealed surprise or a confirmed figure, without treating a secret as guaranteed.
