A favourite that fits the space.

  1. Match name and size

    Copy the exact product name and size from the official listing. Compare stated dimensions with the space where you will keep or gift it.

  2. Check the retailer

    Begin with the official site and its Store Locator. A shop using the brand name or product photos is not, by itself, proof of authorization.

  3. Record what you bought

    Save the product reference, purchase date and your actual cost in a collection ledger. A sold-out listing alone does not establish rarity or resale value.
