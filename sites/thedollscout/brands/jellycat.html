Check plush size & fit

Jellycat / SPACE PLANNER

Will it fit your favourite spot?

Measure the plush in the pose you plan to display. Include ears, tails and limbs without squeezing it.

Editable example measurements, not brand product specifications.

Use centimetres for every measurement.

Your available space
One plush in its intended display pose

Example: a 60 × 30 × 40 cm space with 20 × 15 × 25 cm plush footprints and 2 cm gaps fits 3 in one row when turned 90°.

How the estimate works

This is a uniform rectangular grid, not a maximum mixed-orientation packing plan. No stacking, weight or stability check. Gaps apply between pieces, not at the shelf edges. Measure usable inner space and allow any extra top or edge clearance yourself.

For soft toys, pose and shape can change the required space. Do not assume a named size is the same across characters. This estimates display space, not packaging, safe storage or product suitability.

Check the exact product dimensions at the official source

A favourite that fits the space.

  1. Match name and size

    Copy the exact product name and size from the official listing. Compare stated dimensions with the space where you will keep or gift it.

  2. Check the retailer

    Begin with the official site and its Store Locator. A shop using the brand name or product photos is not, by itself, proof of authorization.

  3. Record what you bought

    Save the product reference, purchase date and your actual cost in a collection ledger. A sold-out listing alone does not establish rarity or resale value.
