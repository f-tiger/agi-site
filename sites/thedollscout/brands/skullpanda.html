Model and limits

Regular-style mode assumes one secret replaces a regular style and all K regular styles have equal probability: p = (1 − 1/N) ÷ K. Enter K and the printed secret denominator N. This assumption is not a verified brand rate. For several secret variants, use the exact per-style percentage instead of a combined secret rate. Use the exact per-style percentage when available.

If your package states a probability for the exact style, choose “Enter a known per-style probability”.

Independent draws only. A sealed no-repeat case or a POP NOW box with hints needs different information. Missing a style earlier does not make the next box luckier. Set a budget before buying.

How the estimate works

This is a uniform rectangular grid, not a maximum mixed-orientation packing plan. No stacking, weight or stability check. Gaps apply between pieces, not at the shelf edges. Measure usable inner space and allow any extra top or edge clearance yourself.

Collect the design you actually want.

  1. Compare formats

    The official Image Of Reality listing is a figure series. Do not use its dimensions or box contents for a similarly named plush pendant.

  2. Choose surprise or certainty

    A blind box offers a random style. If you want one specific character, compare the total cost and seller evidence for a confirmed item.

  3. Plan the display

    Measure the widest point, base and height of each piece. Leave clearance rather than filling every centimetre of a shelf.
