Labubu checker: run the 8 checks, get a straight answer.

The same 8 sourced authenticity checks as our guide — but as a tool: answer what you see on your figure, and the verdict names exactly which checks failed. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded. Last updated 2026-08-30.

🔍 LAFUFU CHECKER

1. Teeth count

Authentic: exactly nine distinct teeth with visible gaps. Counterfeit tell: wrong count, fused teeth, or a single white blob.

2. Face finish

Authentic: soft peach tone, airbrushed blush, glossy eye depth. Counterfeit tell: harsh pink/orange, solid-circle blush, flat dull eyes.

3. Box finish

Authentic: matte box, muted pastels, embossed series name, clean print. Counterfeit tell: glossy, uneven, washed-out or over-saturated print.

4. Anti-counterfeit seal

Authentic: silver scratch label + QR; scratch, scan (it should open fwsy.popmart.com) and enter the code — a valid code confirms. Counterfeit tell: no seal, a QR landing on a lookalike domain, or a code that comes back invalid / already used — check the domain in your browser's address bar.

5. Figure markings

Authentic: Pop Mart engraving / artist name on the foot on 2024+ releases; newer editions also show a UV-reactive mark under the right foot with a UV torch. Counterfeit tell: missing or mushy markings on recent series, or no glow / a smudge under UV where the series should have one. On older series, absence is a question, not a verdict — answer “can't tell”.

6. Build quality

Authentic: even fur, clean seams, crisp paint edges, substantial feel. Counterfeit tell: rough seams, grainy patches, mismatched gloss, light hollow plastic.

7. Price floor

The one check you can run before buying: brand-new “authentic” stock far below official retail is the classic counterfeit signal. The official listing is the price reference.

8. Seller of record

The strongest check of all: an official Pop Mart channel (site, app, stores, Robo Shops, official TikTok Shop, official Amazon storefront) outranks every cosmetic check above.

0 red flag(s)

Failed checks:

Treat this as a counterfeit signal, not a maybe. If you haven't bought yet: don't. If you have: document what you found (photos of the failed checks) and pursue the platform's refund path — a “not as described” claim is exactly what this evidence supports. Full reasoning per check: the guide.

Inconclusive

Too many checks unanswered to say anything honest. The two highest-value checks to resolve: scan the QR seal (check 4) and identify the seller of record (check 8) — the seller check outranks every cosmetic one.

No red flags found

That is not a guarantee. No cosmetic check is proof — counterfeits improve constantly, and Pop Mart's own current guidance outranks this checker where they disagree. What a clean run does mean: none of the eight publicly documented tells is present. The structural protection remains the channel: buy where Pop Mart is the seller.
