Display cases & risers

Choose between an enclosed case, an open riser and the shelf you already own. Inside dimensions and access matter more than a claimed figure count.

What to measure

  1. Measure the widest point, depth and total height of each figure, including its base and projecting parts. A series name is not a size specification.
  2. Measure usable inside width, depth and height. Subtract hinges, door travel and shelf supports; leave room to put a hand in and lift the figure out.
  3. For a riser, check every tier’s depth, height and support surface. The tallest figure on the top tier must still clear the enclosure.

When to skip the purchase

If the usable inside measurements are unknown, a lid cannot clear the tallest piece, or the shelf is already stable and sufficient, pause the purchase. “Fits 20 figures” is not a substitute for dimensions.

IKEA BAGGEBO: glass door, mesh sides

Article 805.029.98 combines a glass door with mesh construction. It is not a sealed dust barrier. Compare enclosure construction, and follow the anchoring instructions. Overall dimensions on a listing are not usable shelf dimensions.

Before paying

  • Compare internal measurements rather than the headline outside dimensions. Ask the seller if only the outside size is given.
  • Check stated load limits, assembly, wall-anchoring instructions and the intended support surface. A fit calculation does not prove stability or safety.
  • Check whether risers, shelves and fixing hardware are included. Compare the delivered total and the return cost for bulky items.
