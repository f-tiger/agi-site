Privacy

We set no tracking cookies. Analytics on this site are:

  • Google Analytics 4 in cookieless mode (client storage disabled, Google signals and ad personalisation off) — page and event counts only; returning visitors look like new ones by design.
  • A first-party beacon that records the page path, the referrer reduced to a hostname, and outbound affiliate clicks reduced to a target hostname — no cookies, no identifiers, no personal data.

We collect no names, no emails, no accounts. Outbound links go to the sites they name; their privacy practices are their own.

Embedded videos: some pages embed YouTube videos in YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). No YouTube cookies are set by loading the page; when you press play, the video streams from YouTube and Google's own privacy practices apply from that point.
