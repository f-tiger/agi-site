Privacy

Google Analytics starts by default on public pages unless you have opted out, and uses analytics cookies. Basic first-party counts do not use cookies. Analytics on this site are:

  • Google Analytics 4 — public pages start analytics by default, while respecting a stored opt-out, Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control. It uses analytics cookies; Google signals and ad personalisation are off. Use the footer analytics toggle to disable or enable analytics. Public page addresses omit query strings and fragments, and referrers are reduced to their origin. Selected files and entered text are not sent. Fixed actions and recognised campaign tags may be included; raw URL parameters are excluded. New and historical pages preserve the same explicit opt-out choice.
  • A first-party beacon records a date, public page path, page language, coarse country code, fixed action and a referrer or affiliate destination hostname. These counts do not add a user identifier or analytics cookie. Collection contents, entered text and exported files are not included. Known tests and supported privacy opt-outs are excluded.

The local collection tools do not require your name, email or an account. Optional account and membership features are separate from these local tools. Outbound links go to the sites they name; their privacy practices are their own.

Embedded videos: homepage video preview images are served by YouTube when they enter view, which sends connection information to its image servers. Homepage and new tool tutorial pages load a YouTube privacy-enhanced player (youtube-nocookie.com) only after you select “Load YouTube player.” Loading it sends connection information to YouTube and Google; their privacy practices then apply. Privacy-enhanced mode does not guarantee anonymous viewing. Historical pages may load an embedded player when the page opens.

Collection tracker and cards

The collection tracker stores the figure names, notes, list status and quantities you enter in this browser’s local storage. We do not upload this collection or its JSON backups and CSV exports. Series PNG cards are also generated locally, after you request a preview. A card contains the official series names and the owned quantities shown in that preview; it excludes prices, personal notes and wishlist entries. Downloading a card does not publish it. If you share the image elsewhere, that service and its audience can see the card’s contents. Clearing browser data removes the local collection; export a JSON backup first. Fixed aggregate events record actions such as save, download initiation, import or calculate, without collection content or measurement values. Existing site analytics described above still apply.

Wanted and trade cards

The wanted-card tool stores one draft in this browser, including your item, general region, budget and currency, condition, explicit trade offer, expiry and your own response status. TDS does not upload or publish these fields. Copying or downloading a card is your choice; people can see its contents when you share it elsewhere. Do not enter private contact details. Delete the local draft with the tool’s delete button; this does not retract shared copies. Fixed anonymous counters distinguish generation, export, self-reported response and self-reported completion. These counters do not verify people, messages, payments or trades.

MCP tool measurement

The public MCP server cannot read your local collection or private files. For an accepted, successfully executed tool call, it can record the date, a fixed tool name and a fixed result category. It does not store the tool arguments, request ID, IP address or full user-agent string in this measurement. Known self-checks and supported privacy signals are excluded. Copying the MCP address can record a fixed copy action after the clipboard operation succeeds. These counts measure operations, not unique people, installations, purchases or revenue.
