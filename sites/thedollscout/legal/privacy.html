Privacy policy

Last updated: July 2026 · In this product category, privacy is not boilerplate — it's the product. This policy is deliberately short and human-readable.

What we collect

  • No account, and no cookies. Browsing this site requires no account, and we set no cookies at all — none for advertising, none for cross-site tracking, none for analytics.
  • Age confirmation: stored only in your own browser (localStorage) so we don't re-ask. It never leaves your device.
  • Anonymous usage statistics. We run Google Analytics 4 in cookieless mode to see which pages and tools people find useful. Because storage is disabled, no identifier is written to your device and we cannot recognise you across visits or across sites. It records the page you viewed and whether a tool was used — for example that a visitor finished the finder or clicked through to a vendor — never who did. Your IP is anonymised, Google Signals and ad personalisation are switched off, and nothing loads at all until you pass the 18+ gate. Details below.
  • Email address — only if you voluntarily subscribe to the checklist/alerts. Used solely to send those emails, from a discreet unbranded sender. Unsubscribe with one click; we delete on request. Your address is never passed to analytics.
  • Hosting logs: our host (Cloudflare) processes standard technical logs (IP, user agent) to serve and secure the site.

What we never do

  • Sell, rent, or share your email or any personal data.
  • Run advertising or retargeting pixels. The analytics above are measurement only; the ad-related features of Google's tag are explicitly disabled.
  • Send your quiz answers, the prices you type into the cost calculator, or your Scam-Check ticks anywhere. Those are computed in your browser. What is counted is deliberately coarse: that a finder was completed and which of four categories it landed on, that the calculator was run and which material/destination were chosen, and the Scam-Check total out of ten — never the individual answers, never a price.
  • Reveal in any email subject line what this site is about — subjects are neutral by design.

Why there's no cookie banner

Because there is nothing to consent to. Consent requirements under the EU/UK ePrivacy rules attach to storing or reading information on your device; our analytics are configured with browser storage switched off, so nothing is stored or read. If that ever changes, a banner appears before any tracking does.

Third parties

Links to vendors (e.g. yourdoll.com, amazon.com) include a referral code identifying that the visit came from this site — not who you are. Once on a vendor's site, their privacy policy applies.

Your rights

Email us to access or delete any data we hold about you (in most cases: just your email subscription). EU/UK visitors have GDPR rights; California visitors have CCPA rights — both honored on request.
