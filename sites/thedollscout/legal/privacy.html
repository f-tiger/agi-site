Privacy

Optional Google Analytics uses cookies only after your consent. Basic first-party counts do not use cookies. Analytics on this site are:

  • Google Analytics 4 — the current document and utility pages load it only after you allow analytics. It uses analytics cookies; Google signals and ad personalisation are off. Use Analytics settings in the footer to change or withdraw your choice. Public page addresses omit query strings and fragments, and referrers are reduced to their origin. Selected files and entered text are not sent. Historical collector pages use the older GA4 loader and may also set analytics cookies; they must not be described as cookieless.
  • A first-party beacon that records the page path, the referrer reduced to a hostname, and outbound affiliate clicks reduced to a target hostname — no cookies, no identifiers, no personal data.

We collect no names, no emails, no accounts. Outbound links go to the sites they name; their privacy practices are their own.

Embedded videos: homepage video preview images are served by YouTube when they enter view, which sends connection information to its image servers. Homepage and new tool tutorial pages load a YouTube privacy-enhanced player (youtube-nocookie.com) only after you select “Load YouTube player.” Loading it sends connection information to YouTube and Google; their privacy practices then apply. Privacy-enhanced mode does not guarantee anonymous viewing. Historical pages may load an embedded player when the page opens.

Collection tracker

The collection tracker stores the figure names, notes, list status and quantities you enter in this browser’s local storage. We do not upload this collection or its JSON backups and CSV exports. Clearing browser data removes the local copy; export a JSON backup first. Aggregate events record actions such as save, export, import or calculate, without collection content or measurement values. Existing site analytics described above still apply.
