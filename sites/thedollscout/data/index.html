The dataset, not just the conclusions

Recorded 11 August 2026 · 53 rows · CC BY 4.0

Every guide in this category tells you what dolls cost and how much they weigh. None of them show you the rows. Here are ours — the same 53 listings our price and weight findings are computed from, as JSON and CSV, free to download, check, disagree with, or reuse commercially.

Why this page exists. This site claims every number is sourced or marked unverified. A claim like that is worth nothing if the numbers only exist inside our own prose. Publishing the dataset is how the claim becomes checkable — including by anyone who wants to prove us wrong.

What is in it

FieldMeaning
titleVendor's product title, verbatim
urlProduct page — no referral parameters on these
heightCmVendor-stated height, centimetres
weightKg / weightLbVendor-stated weight
priceUsdListed price on the recording date, including any sale price
isPartialBodyTrue for torsos and other partial bodies

50 rows are full-size dolls, 3 are partial bodies. Prices span $899 to $2,866. What we concluded from them: the price bands and what changed between scrapes, and why height barely predicts weight.

What is wrong with it

Stated here rather than buried, because a dataset that hides its weaknesses is marketing:

  • Vendor-stated, not independently measured. We did not weigh anything. Repeated models returned identical figures across separate listings, which is what real specification data looks like — but it is still the vendor's number.
  • One vendor. This is a sample of one catalogue, not of the market. Treat cross-vendor generalisations as unsupported.
  • Prices drift. Sale prices are included as displayed. The bands are durable; the exact figures are a snapshot.
  • No material column, deliberately. The collection could not reliably tell TPE from silicone on these listings. A material column would have been a guess wearing a data label, so there isn't one. This is the omission we are most often asked about.
  • Editorial exclusions. Listings under 140 cm are excluded unless explicitly a partial body — a safety rule, not a data-quality one. Accessory listings are excluded because their pages carry the spec table of the doll they fit, which once produced a spurious full-size row at accessory pricing.

If you find an error

Tell us. We have already corrected one of our own published numbers in public — our torso weight range was wrong, the data said so, and the guide now says we corrected it. That is the standard this dataset is published under.
