🔎 MODEL LOOKUP

No match in our index. That does not mean it doesn't exist — collector directories list many more series and characters than we have verified. The authoritative directory is Pop Mart's own The Monsters collection; our index covers only what we've verified against named sources.

Labubu — the character

The main character of The Monsters cast by Kasing Lung: mischievous elf, nine teeth, no tail. The nine-teeth count is also authenticity check #1 (the guide). Sources: SNKRDUNK Magazine; Bagaholicboy; the character coverage linked from the glossary.

Zimomo — the character

The tribe's leader figure: notably larger than Labubu, with a spiked tail — the tail is the fastest visual separator, since Labubu has none. Frequently confused with Labubu in resale listings; a “big Labubu with a tail” is a Zimomo. Sources: SNKRDUNK Magazine; Bagaholicboy; Extrabux comparison.

Mokoko — the character

Pink fur, white belly, heart-shaped nose — one of Labubu's companions, appearing only in select releases. Sources: SNKRDUNK Magazine; LatestLY character guide; Extrabux comparison.

Pato, Spooky & other cast names

Additional named members of The Monsters cast, attested in character guides (LatestLY; THISTOKYO directory). Their individual traits we have not verified — for identification, the official listing of the specific release is the reference.

Have a Seat — the series

Seated vinyl-plush blind box series; commonly reported secret odds for 6-figure series are 1:72 (box-printed rate is authoritative). The official listing is the reference for lineup and price.

Big Into Energy — the series

Vinyl-plush pendant series (April 2025) with six emotion-named regulars — Love, Happiness, Loyalty, Serenity, Hope, Luck — plus secret ID (Prism News catalog; product-insights coverage). The official listing is the reference.

Classic Sparkly — the series

Sparkly plush-pendant take on the classic series. The official listing is the reference.

Exciting Macaron — the series

A frequently referenced vinyl-plush series in collector guides. We have not verified a current official listing — search the official store for live availability.

Vinyl plush pendant — the format

The dominant Labubu format: plush body, vinyl face, bag loop. Format is printed on the official listing and matters for authenticity (fur and face are two of the 8 checks). Full format table: the first-buy guide.

Secret / chase — the term

The hidden design outside the open lineup, most commonly reported at 1:72 (6-figure series) and 1:144 (12-figure) — box-printed rate authoritative. Math and sources: the rarity page; definition: the glossary.

Lafufu — the term

The community's name for a counterfeit Labubu (Gulf News). Identification: the interactive checker or the 8-point guide.

The Monsters — the IP

The cast and world created by artist Kasing Lung, produced under exclusive license by Pop Mart. Labubu is its main character. Source: Wikipedia; SNKRDUNK Magazine.
