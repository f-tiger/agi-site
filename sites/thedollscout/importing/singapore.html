Importing / Singapore

Is it legal to import an adult doll into Singapore?

Not on any prohibited list and no statute bans it — but an officer can still classify it as an obscene article. A grey zone, not a safe harbour. Duty and import-tax treatment is set out below with its source. Childlike dolls are prohibited regardless, with serious criminal penalties. Verify with customs in Singapore before ordering; this is not legal advice.

General information for adult buyers, not legal advice. Rules change — verify current requirements with Singapore customs before ordering.

Status: A plain adult doll imported for genuine personal use is most likely not prohibited, but this is a grey zone rather than a safe harbour. Penal Code section 292 criminalises importing an obscene object for the purposes of sale, hire, distribution, public exhibition or circulation — personal use sits outside that purpose element. Singapore Customs’ prohibited list enumerates obscene articles, publications, videotapes, videodiscs and software, and does not separately name dolls. What remains is officer discretion at inspection. That adult products are sold openly in Singapore retail suggests the discretion is not exercised aggressively, but it exists.

What will you pay in tax and duty?

GST is 9%, raised from 8% on 1 January 2024. Singapore levies customs duty on only four categories — liquor, tobacco, motor vehicles and petroleum — so a doll attracts no duty. The low-value goods threshold is S$400 CIF for goods arriving by air or post. A common misstatement is that the S$400 relief was simply removed in 2023; what actually happened is narrower. From 1 January 2023 the Overseas Vendor Registration regime was extended to low-value goods, so a GST-registered overseas vendor charges 9% at checkout, while import GST relief at the border still applies below S$400 where the supplier is not so registered. Goods above S$400 have always been assessed at the border. A full-size doll exceeds S$400, so the practical rule is 9% GST at the border and no duty.

What does customs actually do with these shipments?

We found no enforcement case against an adult-form doll. The exposure is officer discretion rather than a listed prohibition, which puts the risk somewhere between low and moderate.

What is prohibited regardless of anything else?

Prohibited. Law firm sources cite a Penal Code provision introduced by the Criminal Law Reform Act 2019 prohibiting possession, sale and importation of child sex dolls, defined as anatomically accurate dolls resembling a person under 16 and designed for sexual purposes. We could not open the statute to confirm the section number, so we cite the prohibition rather than a number.

Practical notes for buyers

  • The effective age gate here is 21, not 18. Selling or distributing an obscene object to a person under 21 is a separate offence, even though contractual majority is 18.
  • Do not import for resale. That falls squarely inside the purpose element that makes section 292 bite.
