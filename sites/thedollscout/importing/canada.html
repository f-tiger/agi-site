Importing / Canada

Is it legal to import an adult doll into Canada?

Legal unless classified as obscene under Tariff Item 9899.00.00 — and the CAD 20 threshold that guides routinely miss means China-origin shipments are always taxable. Duty and import-tax treatment is set out below with its source. Childlike dolls are prohibited regardless, with serious criminal penalties. Verify with customs in Canada before ordering; this is not legal advice.

General information for adult buyers, not legal advice. Rules change — verify current requirements with Canada customs before ordering.

Status: Adult-form dolls are not a prohibited category. Importation is lawful for a private adult unless the goods fall within Tariff Item 9899.00.00, which bars obscene material and child pornography. CBSA Memorandum D9-1-1 sets out how that classification is made, and notably defines “sex” for these purposes to include illustrations and animation — which is the doctrinal route by which a three-dimensional object can be brought within the item at all.

What will you pay in tax and duty?

The threshold that matters is not the one usually quoted. Under CUSMA, courier shipments from the US or Mexico are duty-free below CAD 150 and tax-free below CAD 40. For courier shipments from anywhere else — including China, where nearly every doll ships from — the threshold is CAD 20 for both duty and tax, which in practice means everything is assessable. CBSA also does not permit splitting an order across shipments to stay under a threshold, so a doll plus accessories counts as one value. Shipments above CAD 3,300 fall outside the simplified Low Value Shipment programme and need a standard commercial entry, which several premium dolls will exceed.

What does customs actually do with these shipments?

CBSA can detain and seize goods it believes fall under 9899.00.00, with the determination procedure — and the redetermination route — set out in Memorandum D9-1-17. One genuinely useful mechanism almost no other guide mentions: where admissibility is genuinely uncertain, an importer can submit a sample in advance to CBSA’s Prohibited Importations Unit. Retailers and practitioners report that officers assess appearance discretionarily on height, proportions and facial features; that is consistent with the published framework but it is not itself a CBSA statement, so we flag it as reported rather than official.

What is prohibited regardless of anything else?

Prohibited via Tariff Item 9899.00.00 and Criminal Code s. 163.1. The leading matter is R. v. Harrisson in the Newfoundland and Labrador Provincial Court, believed to be Canada’s first trial on child-pornography charges involving a doll: the doll was intercepted by CBSA at the border, the judge found that it was child pornography, but acquitted in June 2019 because the Crown had not proved the accused intended to buy a doll resembling a child. That is a trial-level decision on knowledge and intent; we found no appellate authority, so the point should be treated as unsettled above the trial level rather than as decided law.

Practical notes for buyers

  • The CAD 20 versus CAD 150 distinction is the most common error in Canadian doll guides. The CUSMA numbers only apply to goods originating in the US or Mexico.
  • Order-splitting is expressly not permitted. Value must reflect the whole order even if items ship separately.
  • The advance-sample route to the Prohibited Importations Unit exists if you genuinely cannot tell whether an item is admissible.
  • Interception happens at the border, not in the home. In the Harrisson matter the doll was caught in transit.

Other destinations we've checked

United Kingdom

No prohibition identified on adult-form dolls. Importing a childlike doll is prosecuted — though simple possession is still not an offence.

Germany

Adult-form dolls fine. Childlike dolls are criminal to merely possess — and the €150 duty-free threshold was abolished on 1 July 2026.

Japan

Legal, and the home of the industry's premium manufacturers — but Japan is the one country where childlike dolls are also legal, which is a trap for foreign buyers.
