Importing an adult doll, by country

The question buyers ask after they've paid, when they should ask it before. General information, not legal advice — verify current rules with your own customs authority.

DestinationShort answer
AustraliaAdult-form dolls legal to import. Childlike dolls carry up to 15 years — enforcement is active and aggressive.
New ZealandLawful in principle, but the seizure standard is broader and vaguer than Australia's — read before ordering.
United StatesNo federal ban on adult-form dolls — but the $800 duty-free allowance every other guide still quotes was abolished in August 2025.
CanadaLegal unless classified as obscene under Tariff Item 9899.00.00 — and the CAD 20 threshold that guides routinely miss means China-origin shipments are always taxable.
United KingdomNo prohibition identified on adult-form dolls. Importing a childlike doll is prosecuted — though simple possession is still not an offence.
GermanyAdult-form dolls fine. Childlike dolls are criminal to merely possess — and the €150 duty-free threshold was abolished on 1 July 2026.
JapanLegal, and the home of the industry's premium manufacturers — but Japan is the one country where childlike dolls are also legal, which is a trap for foreign buyers.
South KoreaLegal since the customs guideline of December 2022, reaffirmed by the Supreme Court in April 2026 — after years of seizures and litigation.
SingaporeNot on any prohibited list and no statute bans it — but an officer can still classify it as an obscene article. A grey zone, not a safe harbour.
MalaysiaTreat as prohibited. Enforcement is active and seizures are routine — our advice is not to attempt import.
ThailandIllegal to import, with real criminal exposure. Open sale in local markets gives a false sense of safety — the border is the chokepoint.
Universal prohibition: every jurisdiction covered here bans childlike dolls, several with specific criminal statutes. DollScout exclusively covers adult-form products and reports vendors that stock anything otherwise.
