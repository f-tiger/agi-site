Importing / New Zealand

Is it legal to import an adult doll into New Zealand?

Lawful in principle, but the seizure standard is broader and vaguer than Australia's — read before ordering. Duty and import-tax treatment is set out below with its source. Childlike dolls are prohibited regardless, with serious criminal penalties. Verify with customs in New Zealand before ordering; this is not legal advice.

General information for adult buyers, not legal advice. Rules change — verify current requirements with New Zealand customs before ordering.

Status: Adult-form dolls are lawful in principle, but New Zealand's framing deserves more caution than Australia's. There is no doll-specific statute. Instead, section 95 of the Customs and Excise Act 2018 prohibits importing objectionable publications and other indecent or obscene articles, and New Zealand Customs' own public guidance warns that bringing in "sex toys or dolls that are considered to be indecent or obscene" can result in seizure and prosecution. That wording is not limited to childlike dolls, and the assessment sits with Customs. Every documented seizure we found involved childlike features, and we found no confirmed case of a genuinely adult-form doll being seized — but the guidance permits it, so do not treat the position as settled.

What will you pay in tax and duty?

GST is 15%. The low-value threshold is NZD 1,000 per consignment, measured on the price paid excluding transport, insurance, GST and duty. Since 1 December 2019, Customs does not collect GST or duty at the border on consignments at or below that value; instead, offshore suppliers exceeding NZD 60,000 in annual New Zealand consumer sales must register and charge 15% at checkout. Above NZD 1,000 — or where Customs lacks evidence GST was paid — Customs collects at the border and issues a demand notice before release. From 1 April 2026 a per-consignment Goods Management Levy also applies, including to low-value imports, at roughly NZD 2.21 by air or NZD 2.09 by sea plus GST.

What does customs actually do with these shipments?

New Zealand seizes fewer dolls than Australia, and Customs has publicly said this is because its powers are narrower — it is limited to full-unit dolls where Australia and the UK also seize parts, and it has lobbied for stronger law. Reported figures put the total at roughly seven border seizures and four post-border seizures of full-unit dolls since 2017. Penalty exposure is serious: importing objectionable publications carries up to 14 years, and possession of an item classified objectionable carries up to 10 years.

What is prohibited regardless of anything else?

Prohibited, by an indirect route. Customs seizes the item under the Customs and Excise Act 2018, the Classification Office then classifies it as objectionable under the Films, Videos, and Publications Classification Act 1993, and possession of a classified objectionable item becomes an offence carrying up to 10 years. A 2019 Timaru case followed exactly this sequence and produced an 18-month sentence.

Practical notes for buyers

  • The vague standard is the risk here. Australia tells you plainly that childlike dolls are prohibited. New Zealand tells you that dolls considered indecent or obscene may be seized, and leaves the judgement to Customs. Buy clearly adult-proportioned, and keep the specification sheet.
  • Customs has publicly identified evasion tactics — shipping in parts, fitting adult-sized busts, and labelling items as petite adult figures — which is precisely why small-stature adult dolls draw attention.
  • Most full-size dolls exceed NZD 1,000, so expect border assessment rather than checkout GST, plus the new per-consignment levy from April 2026.
  • Older sources citing the Customs and Excise Act 1996 are out of date. That Act was repealed and replaced by the 2018 Act.

Other destinations we've checked

United States

No federal ban on adult-form dolls — but the $800 duty-free allowance every other guide still quotes was abolished in August 2025.

Canada

Legal unless classified as obscene under Tariff Item 9899.00.00 — and the CAD 20 threshold that guides routinely miss means China-origin shipments are always taxable.

United Kingdom

No prohibition identified on adult-form dolls. Importing a childlike doll is prosecuted — though simple possession is still not an offence.
