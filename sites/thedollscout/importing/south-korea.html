Importing / South Korea

Is it legal to import an adult doll into South Korea?

Legal since the customs guideline of December 2022, reaffirmed by the Supreme Court in April 2026 — after years of seizures and litigation. Duty and import-tax treatment is set out below with its source. Childlike dolls are prohibited regardless, with serious criminal penalties. Verify with customs in South Korea before ordering; this is not legal advice.

General information for adult buyers, not legal advice. Rules change — verify current requirements with South Korea customs before ordering.

Status: Legal for adult-form dolls. Getting here took seven years of litigation. Korea Customs formerly suspended clearance of full-body dolls under Customs Act Article 234(1) as goods harming public morals. The Supreme Court ruled against that position in June 2019, holding that a realistic adult doll is not such an item, but customs continued suspending shipments and importers kept suing — and mostly winning — with more than a thousand dolls reportedly held at one point. Korea Customs formally lifted the ban on 26 December 2022 with revised Real Doll Import Clearance Guidelines, and the Supreme Court reaffirmed the position in April 2026.

What will you pay in tax and duty?

VAT is 10% on imports. The personal-use de minimis is USD 150, or USD 200 for goods of US origin under the Korea–US agreement. A doll exceeds both, so expect duty plus 10% VAT on the full value. Personal imports also require a Personal Customs Clearance Code, which you must obtain before ordering.

What does customs actually do with these shipments?

Clearance is now the norm for a standard adult doll, but three things still get shipments stopped. Youthful styling is assessed on overall appearance including height, weight, face and voice, so petite or young-faced models are the practical danger zone. Dolls made to resemble a specific identifiable person are expressly barred. And dolls with built-in electrical functions such as heating, voice or massage trigger a separate safety-certification requirement. The April 2026 ruling was explicit that customs cannot refuse clearance on external appearance alone — it must examine the purpose of import, the user, and the environment and manner of use.

What is prohibited regardless of anything else?

Prohibited. The Supreme Court held in November 2021 that a device realistically modelled on the physical appearance of a female minor treats children as sexual objects and is therefore goods harming public morals under Article 234. The 2022 customs guideline codified this, barring minor-form dolls assessed on height, weight, face and voice.

Practical notes for buyers

  • Get your Personal Customs Clearance Code before you order, not after the parcel arrives.
  • Avoid celebrity or real-person likeness commissions. Korea expressly bars them, and no other jurisdiction we looked at is as explicit.
  • Consider ordering without heating or voice electronics to avoid the separate safety-certification path.
