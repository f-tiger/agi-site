Importing / Malaysia

Is it legal to import an adult doll into Malaysia?

Treat as prohibited. Enforcement is active and seizures are routine — our advice is not to attempt import. Duty and import-tax treatment is set out below with its source. Childlike dolls are prohibited regardless, with serious criminal penalties. Verify with customs in Malaysia before ordering; this is not legal advice.

General information for adult buyers, not legal advice. Rules change — verify current requirements with Malaysia customs before ordering.

Status: High legal risk; treat as effectively prohibited. Penal Code section 292 covers sale, hire, distribution, public exhibition, circulation and importation of obscene objects, and Malaysian authorities routinely classify adult products under it. The Customs Act 1967 empowers prohibition orders whose schedules list indecent and obscene articles, and the Printing Presses and Publications Act is also used as an enforcement hook. “Obscene” is not statutorily defined, which cuts both ways: there is no bright-line ban on dolls specifically, but there is also no safe harbour for an importer. A full-size doll is conspicuous and hard to characterise as anything else.

What will you pay in tax and duty?

Largely academic given the legal position. For completeness: a 10% sales tax applies to low-value goods at or below RM500 sold online and imported by land, sea or air, effective 1 January 2024, with sellers registering above an annual sales threshold; goods above RM500 attract normal import duty and sales tax at the border.

What does customs actually do with these shipments?

High seizure risk, and this is not theoretical. The Home Ministry seized 2,295 units of adult products worth roughly RM70,000 in raids across Johor, Sarawak, Penang and Perak between mid-January and early February 2024, investigated under the publications legislation and Penal Code section 292. Selangor Customs seized contraband worth over RM700,000 including adult products in June 2024.

What is prohibited regardless of anything else?

Prohibited, as everywhere else on this site. Given that adult products themselves are treated as obscene objects here, a childlike doll compounds an already serious position.
