Importing / United Kingdom

Is it legal to import an adult doll into the United Kingdom?

No prohibition identified on adult-form dolls. Importing a childlike doll is prosecuted — though simple possession is still not an offence. We could not verify current duty figures to this page's standard, so none are published. Childlike dolls are prohibited regardless, with serious criminal penalties. Verify with customs in the United Kingdom before ordering; this is not legal advice.

What does customs actually do with these shipments?

Border Force has referred roughly 230 suspected childlike dolls to police since September 2016. Reported prosecutions share a pattern worth understanding: a parcel from Hong Kong or China is intercepted at an airport, frequently declared as a “mannequin”, and the doll charge is then paired with indecent-images offences after devices are examined. Sentences have ranged from suspended terms to two years eight months.

What is prohibited regardless of anything else?

There is no offence of simple possession in England and Wales. Importation is charged as knowingly acquiring possession of goods subject to a prohibition on importing indecent or obscene articles, under s. 42 of the Customs Consolidation Act 1876 together with s. 170(1)(a) of the Customs and Excise Management Act 1979. The CPS test asks whether the doll was created for sexual use and has sufficient characteristics of a child that it is plainly to be looked at and used for that childlike appearance. One open thread: a Child-like Sexual Abuse Dolls (Offences) Bill exists before Parliament and we could not establish its current status, so a dedicated possession offence may now exist or be imminent. Check before relying on the possession position.

Practical notes for buyers

  • Mis-declaring a doll as a “mannequin” is the recurring fact pattern in UK prosecutions, and it aggravates rather than helps. Declare accurately.
  • A seizure triggers a wider search. In nearly every reported case the doll charge arrived alongside indecent-images charges found on devices.
