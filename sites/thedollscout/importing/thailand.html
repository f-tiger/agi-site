Importing / Thailand

Is it legal to import an adult doll into Thailand?

Illegal to import, with real criminal exposure. Open sale in local markets gives a false sense of safety — the border is the chokepoint. Duty and import-tax treatment is set out below with its source. Childlike dolls are prohibited regardless, with serious criminal penalties. Verify with customs in Thailand before ordering; this is not legal advice.

General information for adult buyers, not legal advice. Rules change — verify current requirements with Thailand customs before ordering.

Status: Illegal. Criminal Code section 287 covers making, producing, possessing for trade, importing, distributing and trading in obscene objects, carrying up to three years’ imprisonment and a fine, and adult products are interpreted as obscene objects under it. Such goods are prohibited imports under the Customs Act. Reform has been proposed by more than one political party but, as far as we could establish, has not passed.

What will you pay in tax and duty?

VAT is 7%. The former THB 1,500 de minimis was first narrowed by a temporary VAT measure from July 2024 and then, according to secondary reporting, abolished from 1 January 2026, making all imported parcels assessable regardless of value. We could not confirm that final step against the Customs Department directly, so verify it if the figure matters to you — though for this category the legal position makes the tax question moot.

What does customs actually do with these shipments?

Very high seizure risk. Confiscation at the border is the reported norm, and travellers have been warned of imprisonment and fines for bringing in even small personal devices.

What is prohibited regardless of anything else?

Practical notes for buyers

  • The open sale of adult products in Thai markets is the trap. Domestic non-enforcement does not extend to the border, where customs screening is the actual chokepoint.
  • Our advice for Thai readers is not to import. This is the clearest do-not-buy destination on this site.
