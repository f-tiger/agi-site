Where to buy a real Labubu.

Every authenticity check on the 8-point list exists because somebody bought from the wrong place. The channel decides how much checking you owe: where Pop Mart is the seller of record, a counterfeit structurally cannot reach the shelf. Channel list per press coverage of Pop Mart's own advice (ABC News); recorded 2026-08-30.

The short answer: buy where the seller of record is Pop Mart — the official site, the app, retail stores and Robo Shops, the official TikTok Shop, or the official Pop Mart storefront on Amazon. On any other channel, the price is your first tell: far below retail means Lafufu.

What are the official channels for buying Labubu?

Per press coverage of Pop Mart's own guidance: Pop Mart's website and app, Pop Mart retail stores and Robo Shop vending machines, the official Pop Mart TikTok Shop, official Pop Mart booths at events, and the official Pop Mart storefront on Amazon. The common thread is that in every one of these, the seller of record is Pop Mart itself — a counterfeit cannot enter the shelf. Everywhere else, you are trusting an intermediary.

Is buying Labubu on Amazon safe?

The official Pop Mart storefront on Amazon is on the official channel list — buy where the listing's seller is POP MART. Amazon search results, however, mix that storefront with third-party sellers, and a listing's photos prove nothing about the unit that ships. Check the “sold by” line before paying, and treat prices far below official retail as the counterfeit signal they are. If a listing fails the seller check, no photo check rescues it.

Practical route: start from the POP MART storefront, or from a search for “POP MART Labubu The Monsters” — and then apply the seller check to whatever you click. We link searches and the storefront rather than individual listings on purpose: listings churn, and a dead or swapped listing is worse than a search you can judge yourself.

How much does a real Labubu cost?

Standard blind boxes have tracked at roughly $14 to $28 at retail (DemandSage, 2026), with some premium series listed higher on Pop Mart's own store — and sold-out series trading above retail secondhand. Prices move per series and stock cycle, so check the live official listing rather than trusting any fixed number, this page's included. What is stable is the floor logic: brand-new “authentic” stock far below official retail is how counterfeits are priced, not how genuine ones are.

Current series to price-check against the official store: Have a Seat, Big Into Energy, Classic Sparkly. We deliberately print no exact prices here — the official page is the price, and a guide that hard-codes one goes stale the day a series rotates.

Where do most fake Labubus get sold?

Wherever the seller of record is not Pop Mart and the price undercuts retail: marketplace third-party listings, social-media shops, bulk “wholesale” offers, and street stalls. Sources covering Pop Mart's advice give the same guidance from the other direction: the company recommends buying only through official channels or verified retailers. On any other channel, assume the 8-point check is your only protection, and that it can lose.
