Labubu secret odds for six boxes

Six boxes — one of each regular? Six is the number of regular figures in a standard series, which is why so many people buy six and expect the set. Last updated 2026-08-31.

8.0% chance of at least one secret from six boxes — at 1:72 printed odds

The claim this box count disproves

Circulating: “Buy six and you get the complete set.”

Actually: Six boxes drawn independently do not produce six different figures — duplicates are the normal outcome, not bad luck. Six boxes buys you six draws, and the secret is a separate question with its own low rate.

The math, in the open

For six boxes drawn independently, the chance of at least one secret is 1 − (1 − 1/N)6 and the expected count is simply 6/N. Across every reported series format:

What that costs — as a multiple, not a price

We do not print prices — they rotate with series and stock. As a multiple: the expected cost of one secret is 72× a single box at 1:72, 144× at 1:144 and 720× at 1:720. Six boxes covers 0.08 of that. Put your own local box price into the calculator on the psychology page.

Quick answers

What are the odds of a secret from six boxes?
At 1:72 printed odds the chance of at least one secret is 8.0%, and the expected number of secrets is 0.08. Rarer formats are lower: 4.1% at 1:144 and 0.8% at 1:720.

How many boxes would a 50 percent chance take?
At 1:72 it takes 50 boxes for 50 percent and 165 for 90 percent. At 1:144 it is 100 and 331; at 1:720 it is 499 and 1657.
