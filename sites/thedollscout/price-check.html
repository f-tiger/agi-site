Price Reality Check

The single strongest counterfeit signal is a price no real vendor can match. This tool quantifies it: your quoted price against 53 verified listings (height, weight and price recorded from a vetted vendor on 2026-08-11, published as an open dataset). Ten seconds, no account.





What this can and cannot tell you — read before trusting any verdict:
  • The bands come from one vetted vendor's catalog on one date (53 rows, recorded 2026-08-11, TPE-heavy). They are a reference floor, not a market census.
  • A normal price does not make a shop legitimate — counterfeiters copy real prices too. A passing price only means price isn't the tell; run the 10-point Scam-Check regardless.
  • A below-band price is not 100% proof of fraud — clearances exist. It means the burden of proof flips: verify factory authorization and pay only with recourse-protected methods.
