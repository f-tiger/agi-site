# Free collector tools

https://thedollscout.com/collector-tools
Content updated: 2026-10-31

A calculator for the next decision: probability, budget, collection progress or display space.

A shareable plan for your next collectible meetup
Make an activity template for your group, or a personal card of the figures you want and explicitly offer. Share one link; each recipient can start their own card.
https://thedollscout.com/collecting/event-kit

Make a wanted or trade card
Tell other collectors exactly what you are looking for. Preview a card, then take it to your own community post.
https://thedollscout.com/collecting/wish-card

Blind boxes or a confirmed figure?
Set a spending limit and compare independent boxes with an all-in quote for the exact figure you want.
https://thedollscout.com/collecting/budget

How many new styles could I add?
Estimate new regular styles and repeats before opening more independent boxes from one series.
https://thedollscout.com/collecting/duplicates

Understand blind-box odds
Calculate independent draws using the odds for the exact series.
https://thedollscout.com/brands/labubu#style-probability

Plan your display
Check dimensions and clearance before choosing a display case.
https://thedollscout.com/brands/jellycat#display-fit

Track your collection
Keep owned figures, a wishlist and duplicate quantities in one private list.
https://thedollscout.com/collection-tracker
