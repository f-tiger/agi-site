Regular-style mode assumes one secret replaces a regular style and all K regular styles have equal probability: p = (1 − 1/N) ÷ K. Enter K and the printed secret denominator N. This assumption is not a verified brand rate. For several secret variants, use the exact per-style percentage instead of a combined secret rate. Use the exact per-style percentage when available.

Keep the release name attached
Mime identifies a specific figure release. Save that full name with the character, photos and seller description so a similar title does not replace your target.

Separate a case from loose boxes
The official Mime listing describes a 12-box non-repeating whole set. Our independent-draw calculator does not predict the contents of that set or its secret allocation.

Budget around your target
