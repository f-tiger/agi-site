Which one are you hoping for?

Calculate the chance of your chosen style before opening another box.

Have a Seat: Dada, Hehe, Baba, Sisi, Zizi, Ququ

Example: 12 independent boxes at secret odds of 1:72 give a 15.45% chance of at least one secret.

The regular-style model assumes the secret replaces a regular style and every regular style has the same chance: p = (1 − 1/N) ÷ K. The example uses K = 6 and N = 72. The official source confirms style names; it does not establish these model assumptions.

If your package states a probability for the exact style, choose “Enter a known per-style probability”.

Independent draws only. A sealed no-repeat case or a POP NOW box with hints needs different information. Missing a style earlier does not make the next box luckier. Set a budget before buying.
