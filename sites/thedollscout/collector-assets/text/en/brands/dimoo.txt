Regular-style mode assumes one secret replaces a regular style and all K regular styles have equal probability: p = (1 − 1/N) ÷ K. Enter K and the printed secret denominator N. This assumption is not a verified brand rate. For several secret variants, use the exact per-style percentage instead of a combined secret rate. Use the exact per-style percentage when available.
