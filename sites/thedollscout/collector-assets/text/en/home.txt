Display cases & risers

Show the figures you already own on a shelf or inside a separate case.

Check before choosing: Compare usable inside space, each riser’s top surface and clearance above the tallest piece. Open risers do not enclose dust.

Skip the purchase when: Your existing shelf is stable, has enough room and gives you the view you want.

Storage & transport

Separate small accessories at home or prepare figures for a short trip.

Check before choosing: Storage and carrying are different jobs. Check compartment space after padding, contact materials and the closure.

Skip the purchase when: Suitable original packaging already holds the pieces securely.

Skip the purchase when: A shelf or basket you own already supports the shape and leaves lifting room.
