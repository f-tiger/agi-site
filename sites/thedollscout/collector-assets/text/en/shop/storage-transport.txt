What to measure

Measure each piece in the orientation it will travel, with delicate ears, hands or accessories included.

Compare clear compartment dimensions after dividers and padding are fitted. Check whether the lid presses on the tallest part.

Measure the loaded box against the bag or vehicle space. Check handles, latches and the stated load limit, not just the empty-box size.

Compare the options

Divided storage box — You want separated pieces in a drawer or cupboard. — Hard dividers can still touch painted surfaces; the material and lining need checking.

Padded carrying case — Pieces need separation during a trip. — Padding can reduce movement but is not a drop-proof promise. Confirm compartment fit and closure.

Original packaging — You already have fitted packaging in good condition. — Inspect inserts, loose parts and closure. Do not assume every retail box is designed for repeated transport.

Before paying

Use the manufacturer’s care information for the exact material. Avoid adhesive, unverified cleaners and padding that may transfer colour onto the finish.

Keep pieces and removable accessories from hitting each other. Do a gentle movement check over a clear surface before carrying.

Confirm the listing’s included dividers, foam or lining, plus replacement options and return terms. “Protective” without a construction description is not enough.

When to skip the purchase

If the original packaging already holds the pieces securely, try it first. Do not buy a generic compartment box when its dimensions or contact materials are unknown; do not force a delicate piece into padding.
