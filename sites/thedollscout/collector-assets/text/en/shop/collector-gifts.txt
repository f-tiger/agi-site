What to measure

Ask for the exact brand, series, character and size, or an explicitly wanted item from their wishlist. An unmarked checklist entry is not a wish.

For an accessory, ask for a shelf measurement and the largest figure’s footprint and height. Do not infer size from a product photograph.

Set a delivered budget that includes postage and possible import costs. Leave room for the recipient’s location and return arrangements.

When to skip the purchase

If you do not know the desired series, format or space, ask first. Share a wishlist or a gift plan instead of buying another random box or an accessory that may not fit.

Compare the options

An exact wanted item — The recipient names a specific character or product. — Confirm format, condition and seller. A sealed random box does not promise that character.

A sealed surprise — The recipient enjoys that exact series and accepts duplicates. — Check age guidance, box format and probability statements; a secret is not guaranteed.

Display or storage accessory — The recipient has asked for one and supplied measurements. — Useful only if it fits their setup. Included parts and return terms matter more than a generic “collector gift” label.

Before paying

Start at the official brand store or regional directory for the toy. A marketplace name or familiar photo alone does not verify the seller.

Check suitability and age guidance on the specific product. Collector figures and small parts are not automatically suitable for young children.

Confirm delivery destination, gift receipt and return deadline with the actual seller. Do not assume holiday extensions or advertised arrival dates apply to every offer.

These are category-selection guides, not hands-on product reviews or live deals. We do not rank untested models, invent prices or certify authenticity. Use the checks below with the actual offer, and keep an existing setup when it already works.
