# Explore all collectible brands

https://thedollscout.com/brands
Source review: 2026-10-02

Choose a collection by its format and the decision you need to make. Each guide includes its own working tools and official reference.

Labubu / THE MONSTERS: Start with the exact series and format. THE MONSTERS figures, vinyl plush and pendants are different products.
https://thedollscout.com/brands/labubu

SKULLPANDA: Explore the design, then check the format. A figure series and a plush pendant series need separate size and contents checks.
https://thedollscout.com/brands/skullpanda

Jellycat: Choose a character, size and use. The same name can appear in several sizes, so compare the full product details before buying.
https://thedollscout.com/brands/jellycat

Sonny Angel: Find the series you like, then use the official regional store and retailer directory to check where you buy.
https://thedollscout.com/brands/sonny-angel

SMISKI: Build a small scene around the poses you like. The official Living Series page identifies six regular poses and a randomly distributed secret; it does not give a numerical secret rate.
https://thedollscout.com/brands/smiski

HIRONO: Use HIRONO Mime as a concrete starting point, then compare the exact piece with the scene you want to build. A whole-set assortment and separate loose boxes need different probability models.
https://thedollscout.com/brands/hirono

DIMOO: Pick a theme before chasing a complete shelf. Animal Kingdom is a figure release; record its exact identity so other DIMOO formats do not get mixed into the same checklist.
https://thedollscout.com/brands/dimoo

MOLLY: Match the full MOLLY format and release. MEGA SPACE MOLLY 100% Series4 lists Basic, Regular, Special and Secret labels; those labels alone do not establish equal probabilities.
https://thedollscout.com/brands/molly
