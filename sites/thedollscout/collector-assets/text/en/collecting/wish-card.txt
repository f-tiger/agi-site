# Make a wanted or trade card

https://thedollscout.com/collecting/wish-card
Content updated: 2026-10-31

Tell other collectors exactly what you are looking for. Preview a card, then take it to your own community post.

What your card can look like

Create a text or PNG card without installing an app. Start with your own details below.

Example — not a real request

Wanted to buy: SMISKI Museum / Smiski Pearl Earring

Country / general region: United States

Maximum budget (including shipping): 25.00 USD

Condition / included parts needed: Opened, undamaged figure; box optional

Explicit trade offer: No trade offer

Valid through: Your chosen date

The example budget is fictional, not a market price or valuation.

This draft stays in this browser. It is not published or matched on TDS. Do not enter your address, phone number or private contact details. People can reply to the post where you choose to share it.

Not proof of ownership or authenticity. For trades, attach your own current proof-of-life photo where the community requires it. Check community rules before posting.

Only your own local record. TDS has not verified a reply, match, payment or completed trade.

Choose the exact item, general region, budget and condition. Only list an offer you are ready to trade.
