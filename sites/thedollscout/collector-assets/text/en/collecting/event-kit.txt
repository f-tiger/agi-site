Make an activity template for your group, or a personal card of the figures you want and explicitly offer. Share one link; each recipient can start their own card.

Activity information is supplied by the person who made this card. TDS has not verified the organizer, event or rules.

Activity details

Only enter what you explicitly want or would offer. Up to 8 names per list, one per line. Missing or duplicate figures are not selected automatically.

Search by series or figure. You can also type names directly below; unknown names stay unresolved.

Only published catalog names are checked. Secrets, variants and other releases may be missing.

No account is needed. This tool does not save drafts or change your collection. The share link contains the activity and chosen figure names as readable data, not an encrypted or revocable secret. Anyone with it can read, change or forward it. Do not include private contact details.

A preparation card is not a registration, match, reply, ownership proof or completed trade. Arrange any response in the place where the link was shared, and confirm the organizer’s current rules.

An organizer prepares an activity template with its own rules. No participant’s collection is included.

Each participant opens that link and chooses their own wanted and offered names.

Review the result, then share it where the activity allows. Recipients can create a separate card without copying your choices.

You may link to this free tool from your own activity information and distribute your reviewed template. Keep the rules specific to your event. This does not create a TDS or brand partnership.
