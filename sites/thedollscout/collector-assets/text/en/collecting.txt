Practical answers with worked examples, explicit limits and sources you can check.

Which blind-box probability model fits?
Use independent-draw math only when each box has the same chance and previous outcomes give no information about the next one.
https://thedollscout.com/collecting/blind-box-rules

Check a toy retailer before paying
Begin with the official regional store or directory, then verify the exact seller and item. Product photos and a familiar logo do not establish authorization.
https://thedollscout.com/collecting/check-retailers

Measure a display that actually fits
Use the empty inside space and the full outline of the object in its intended pose. Outside box dimensions and nominal toy height are not enough.
https://thedollscout.com/collecting/display-checklist

Start a collection you can keep enjoying
Choose a small theme, a spending limit and a place to display it. A clear wish list is more useful than treating every new release as a missing piece.
https://thedollscout.com/collecting/start-a-collection
