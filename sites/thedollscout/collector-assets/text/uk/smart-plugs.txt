Choose a UK smart plug by the appliance, app and energy data you need. Compare Tapo P110 and P110M references without assuming automatic savings.

Start with the appliance and the feature you need: switching, a timer or measured electricity use. Confirm the UK plug and permitted load first. Matter compatibility and energy reporting are separate checks; a smart plug does not save energy merely by being installed.

How to choose

Choose the job before the ecosystem

A lamp timer may need only scheduled switching. Investigating consumption needs energy monitoring and access to the history you require. Write down whether local control, remote access or a particular home platform is essential before choosing a model.

Check the actual integration

Tapo’s UK P110M page describes Matter and energy monitoring but also carries a limitation on energy reporting through Matter. Features can depend on hardware, firmware and the controller. Verify the exact integration instead of assuming every app displays the same readings.

Match the load, not just the headline amperage

The UK P110M source gives a 13A maximum and separate restrictions for motors/compressors and EV charging. Follow the exact device and appliance manuals. Do not use a generic rating as permission to control an unsuitable appliance remotely.

Questions before choosing

Does Matter guarantee energy readings in every app?

No. Check the product’s firmware notes and your controller’s supported functions. A logo alone does not establish that the measurements you need will appear.

Will a smart plug lower my bill?

Only a useful change in appliance operation could reduce consumption. Include the plug’s own use and purchase cost; TDS does not claim a saving for your home.
