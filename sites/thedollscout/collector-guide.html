Collector starter guide

A simple workflow: list what you own, set a limit, measure your space and check the next purchase.

1. Start with what you already own

Add one entry per figure, give duplicates a quantity, and keep future purchases on the wishlist. These are your own records, not an official product catalogue or authenticity certificate.

2. Give the next purchase a limit

Write down a spending limit before browsing. A wishlist is a place to wait, not a shopping obligation. For blind boxes, use the printed odds and our secret odds calculator; opening more boxes never guarantees a secret under the independent-draw model.

3. Measure the display

Measure the usable inside width, depth and height. Include the stand and the widest parts of the figure. Subtract the space taken by hinges and doors before entering the dimensions. Start with the largest footprint you plan to display.

4. Check the channel

Use the buying-channel guide and sourced authenticity checks for Labubu. A checklist cannot certify a figure. Check the manufacturer’s current instructions when details differ.

5. Keep a portable copy

Download a JSON backup after a collection update. You can restore it on another browser. CSV is for spreadsheets; it is not a restore file. Neither file is uploaded by these tools.
