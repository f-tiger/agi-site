# Product marketing context

Version 1 — 2026-09-18. Draft from repository, owner statements and public sources; no invented customer interviews. Read `docs/commercial-skills-review-2026-09-18.md` before extending an experiment. Unknowns below are blockers to investment, not blanks to fill with assumptions.

## Business goal and proof

Owner wants a portfolio that can scale from approximately €11 to €110 and €1,100 in trailing-30-day net revenue. Net revenue means receipts less refunds and tax; profit also deducts provider, payment, acquisition, maintenance and human delivery costs. Eco has the only owner-reported revenue. New experiments have no verified customers, paid retention, CAC or revenue. Prices displayed on experiments are hypotheses; all paid offers are closed.

## Alternatives and objections

- LocaleBatch: Shopify Translate & Adapt and manual/AI translation. Native free auto-translation supports two languages. An additional language is a plausible trigger, but CSV preparation, quality and import risk may outweigh the benefit. We have no comparative merchant delivery results.
- RFQ Desk: an existing spreadsheet and supplier conversations. Experienced buyers may prefer flexibility and manual review; our calculator does not parse messy PDF quotes, evaluate suppliers or create supply. Avoid targeting established sourcing agents by default.
- ModelMeter: provider dashboards, LiteLLM, Langfuse, Helicone and spreadsheets. A free CSV chart is easy to substitute. Native export mapping and recurring reconciliation remain gaps, not built differentiators.
- QuerySprint: SQLBolt, free tutorials and AI tutoring. Three tasks cannot support a complete-course claim. A paid collection needs an observed reason to buy beyond free syntax practice.

## Positioning, voice and evidence

Sell the exact deliverable and state its limitations. Show a working example before asking for data. Explain inputs and the next action for beginners. No fabricated testimonials, savings, certifications, suppliers, employment outcomes or revenue. Use customer language only with a source; our headlines are editorial hypotheses, not customer quotes. Friendly, concrete and task-focused English on these four tools; existing Learn content remains Chinese.

## Distribution and measurement

Existing sites are potential entry points, not proven channels for these products. Link only where the visitor's task matches. Do not assume a Chinese free-resource audience will buy an English SaaS. RFQ has no demonstrated owned acquisition channel. Do not publish promotional community posts or send outreach without authorization.

Separate sample use, own-data use, exercises and QA. Opt-in anonymous page-visit events are not verified people, retained customers or conversion rates. Record negative/zero-use answers as carefully as interest. Ask about the past 30 days, not hypothetical enthusiasm. An interest click is not a lead, reservation or sale.

## Investment rules

No new paid features for the three discovery probes until repeated task evidence and a reachable buyer segment are documented. After 100 relevant landing visits, 20 own-task completions and five contextual price-interest visits, investigate manually; this operating gate is not statistical proof or automatic permission to charge. Missing distribution is an inconclusive market result. Before scaling: three independent actual buyers, positive contribution after delivery/support and repeat buying evidence appropriate to the model. No advertising spend, purchased domains or new recurring provider expense in this release.
